ABOUT THE PUBLISHING HOUSE

ABOUT ZAY-MARIE PRESS

Zay-Marie Press is an independent Christian publishing house committed to producing biblically faithful works that strengthen believers, edify the Church, and contribute to the faithful proclamation of God's Word.

WHY WE EXIST

Books That Strengthen the Church

We publish books that strengthen the Church, not books that merely sell to the Church.

Zay-Marie Press was established to publish works in which biblical fidelity, doctrinal substance, and spiritual usefulness take precedence over passing trends. We believe Christian publishing should do more than place books into the marketplace. It should place trustworthy resources into the hands of believers—resources capable of teaching, strengthening, correcting, encouraging, and equipping.

OUR GOVERNING PRINCIPLES

What Receives the Zay-Marie Press Imprint

Faithful to Scripture

Every Zay-Marie Press publication must be grounded in the authority, clarity, and sufficiency of Scripture. Biblical truth is not adjusted to accommodate cultural trends, theological fashion, or popular sentiment. Our books seek to handle the Word of God carefully, allowing Scripture to govern both doctrine and interpretation.

Led by the Spirit

Sound biblical study requires more than intellectual analysis. We affirm our dependence upon the Holy Spirit for illumination, discernment, and spiritual understanding. Our publications therefore seek to unite careful study of the biblical text with humble submission to the Spirit who inspired it and who works through His truth.

Life-Giving Truth

Biblical doctrine is intended not merely to inform the mind, but to strengthen and establish the believer. We publish truth that nourishes faith, promotes spiritual maturity, and equips the Church. Theological precision and spiritual fruitfulness belong together; sound doctrine should deepen both understanding and faithful Christian living.

Eternal Purpose

Our publishing perspective reaches beyond immediate concerns to the purposes of God revealed in Scripture. We seek to help readers understand God's promises, His present work, our heavenly calling, and the hope set before believers. Each publication should ultimately direct attention beyond ourselves to God's unfolding purpose and eternal glory.

THEOLOGICAL FOUNDATIONS

Defined Boundaries. Broad Biblical Purpose.

Zay-Marie Press publishes within defined theological boundaries. These boundaries provide doctrinal clarity while allowing the press to publish qualifying works across a range of biblical and theological subjects.

OUR PUBLISHING PROGRAMS

Distinct Programs Under One Publishing House

Zay-Marie Press may develop distinct publishing programs and series within its broader mission. Each program may possess its own visual identity and specialized theological focus while remaining under the doctrinal and publishing standards of Zay-Marie Press.

Acts Overlap Theology Series

The Acts Overlap Theology Series is a publishing program devoted to the study of God's Prophecy Program for Israel and His Mystery Program for the Body of Christ, including their concurrent operation during the Acts 9–28 overlap.

Zay-Marie Press is not limited to the Acts Overlap Theology Series.
