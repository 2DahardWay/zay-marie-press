HOW WE READ SCRIPTURE

Four commitments behind every page

These are the habits every study, volume and companion is built on.

The text comes first

We begin with what a passage says, in its own chapter and setting, before we draw any conclusion from it.

Rightly divided

Scripture is addressed to different people at different times. We ask who is being spoken to before we ask how it applies.

Israel and the Body, distinct

Israel’s prophetic program and the Body of Christ’s mystery calling run on their own lines and are not merged.

The King James Bible

Every verse we quote is from the King James Version, so you can check each reading against the same words.

ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Alexander S. Marien

ASM

Alexander S. Marien

Minister · Theologian · Teacher · Author

Alexander S. Marien is a minister, theologian, teacher and author whose work centers on doctrinal clarity, biblical architecture, and pastoral formation. A graduate of Capital Seminary & Graduate School (2018) with a Master of Divinity (M.Div) and advanced graduate studies in pastoral counseling, he writes with a blend of theological precision and shepherding insight.

As the creator of the Acts Overlap Theology Series and the founder of Zay-Marie Press, Marien develops resources that help believers understand Scripture through a structured, rightly divided framework. His studies emphasize the distinction between Israel's prophetic program and the Body of Christ's mystery calling, offering readers a clear, accessible pathway through complex doctrinal themes.

Marien's work reflects a commitment to rigorous exegesis, careful dispensational boundaries, and pastoral application — equipping churches, teachers, and students to handle the Word of Truth with confidence and depth.
