ABOUT ZAY-MARIE PRESS

WHERE THE BIBLE MEANS WHAT IT SAYS.

Zay-Marie Press publishes doctrinally sound, spiritually fruitful books anchored in the authority, clarity, and sufficiency of Scripture. Our purpose is to equip believers, edify the Church, and advance the faithful proclamation of God's Word.

WHY ZAY-MARIE PRESS

“We publish books that strengthen the Church, not books that merely sell to the Church.”

Zay-Marie Press is a biblical-doctrinal publishing house. We prioritize Scripture over sentiment, doctrine over trend, clarity over ambiguity, theological precision over broad appeal, and eternal purpose over market categories. Every book, course, digital study, and free resource we produce is built on the same defined theological foundations.

ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Alexander S. Marien

ASM

Alexander S. Marien

Pastor · Teacher · Author

Alexander S. Marien is a pastor, teacher, and author whose work centers on doctrinal clarity, biblical architecture, and pastoral formation. A seminary graduate with a Doctor of Ministry and advanced graduate studies in pastoral counseling, he writes with a blend of theological precision and shepherding insight.

As the creator of the Acts Overlap Theology Series and the founder of Zay-Marie Press, Marien develops resources that help believers understand Scripture through a structured, rightly divided framework. His studies emphasize the distinction between Israel's prophetic program and the Body of Christ's mystery calling, offering readers a clear, accessible pathway through complex doctrinal themes.

Marien's work reflects a commitment to rigorous exegesis, careful dispensational boundaries, and pastoral application — equipping churches, teachers, and students to handle the Word of Truth with confidence and depth.
