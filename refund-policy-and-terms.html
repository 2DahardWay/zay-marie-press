Refund Policy

Zay-Marie Press products are digital publications delivered electronically immediately after purchase. Because digital files cannot be returned once delivered, all sales are generally considered final except as expressly provided below.

Refund Eligibility

A refund may be requested within 14 days of purchase under the following circumstances:

  • The file is damaged, incomplete, corrupted, or otherwise unusable, and we are unable to resolve the issue by providing a replacement file.
  • You were charged more than once for the same product.
  • You accidentally purchased the wrong edition of a product and have not used or distributed the materials. At our discretion, we may either refund the purchase or apply the purchase price toward the correct edition.

Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds are not provided for:

  • Requests submitted more than 14 days after purchase.
  • Change-of-mind purchases after files have been delivered.
  • Doctrinal disagreement with the author's conclusions or theological positions.
  • Failure to complete a course or publication.
  • Personal preference regarding content, format, difficulty level, or presentation.
  • Purchases made without first reviewing the product description.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact Zay-Marie Press through:

  • Email: info@zaymariepress.com
  • The Contact page on ZayMariePress.com, or
  • The Contact link on our Payhip store.

Please include:

  • The email address used for purchase
  • The product name
  • A brief description of the issue

We strive to respond within three business days.

Approved refunds are issued through the original payment method and may require additional processing time by Payhip or the payment provider.
