# Zay-Marie Press website — instructions for Claude

This repository is the production site for zaymariepress.com. Pushing to `main` deploys automatically through Cloudflare Workers & Pages. Commit and push directly to `main`; no pull requests. The publisher is Hakeem (GitHub: 2DahardWay).

## Rule-sync rule (publisher directive, 2026-10-01 — never skip)

Every rule that is added, changed or modified goes in TWO places: (1) this CLAUDE.md, and (2) the Master Standard (`controlling-documents/Zay-Marie_Press_Digital_Studies_Master_Standard_vX.Y.docx` and `.pdf`). Whenever CLAUDE.md is updated with a rule, the Master Standard is updated in the same pass: edit the DOCX, bump the version, rename both files to match, regenerate the PDF, log the change in the Master Standard's Version History, and deliver the new PDF and DOCX. A rule that exists in only one of the two is incomplete work. Rules that live in a working file (for example the Category Standard) are also written into the Master Standard. Do not push a rule change unless both are updated.

## Reader-facing wording rule (publisher directive, 2026-10-02 — never skip)

Internal working terms never appear in anything the reader sees (study text, website copy, Doctrinal Packets, previews, catalog and connections-guide copy). The Framework, the PAM / Program Assignment Manual, the Master Standard, rule numbers, standing and anchoring sentences, review notes, audit trackers, "firewall" and similar control labels are the publisher's and Claude's working apparatus, not content for readers. Never write "The Framework does not assign …" or "The PAM makes no assignment …" in a study; use "This reading rests on the letter's own (or the passage's own) audience markers and setting." (Master Standard §20 Rules 8, 12 and 14). Never propose explaining the apparatus to the reader; remove the reference instead. In every study check (Step 1), grep for "Framework", "PAM", "Program Assignment", "Master Standard", "firewall", "Rule " and "anchor" and report each hit.

## Preview-page conclusion-spoiler standard (publisher directive, 2026-09-28)

Applies to every "What You Will Learn in This Study" page inside the interior PDF, and to the website's "What You'll Study" (6 points) section on every study page — both are the same preview promise in two places.

- The preview page states the **skills and interpretive outcomes** the reader will gain. It is a value signal, not an answer key: it must promise mastery, not deliver the solution.
- It must never reveal the identity of a figure before the study presents it, give away the doctrinal conclusion, state the interpretive answer outright, collapse the discovery process, or pre-empt the study's narrative arc. (Flagged example of what this replaces: "Identify the male child of 12:5 as the 144,000…" — states the conclusion before the reader begins.)
- Instead, each point describes the *process* the reader will follow, names the *textual markers* they will trace, and states the *interpretive discipline* they will learn. Model (Study 61, point 3): "Trace the identity of the male child in 12:5 by following the chapter's own textual markers — including the term 'caught up' (harpazō) and the later appearance of a sealed firstfruits company in Revelation 14:1–5."
- Applies immediately to Study 61 and to all new studies going forward. Retrofitting the 60 previously completed studies is a separate task; the website side (both the "What You'll Study" bullets and the "A Look Inside" preview image) is now fully COMPLETE and live for all 60 studies. The 10 studies with interior-PDF violations (37, 41, 45, 47, 48, 49, 51, 52, 53, 54) have corrected final PDFs approved and delivered to the publisher, but distribution to existing purchasers is settled (silent swap, decided 2026-09-29) — see `controlling-documents/Preview_Page_Spoiler_Retrofit_Audit_v1.4.md` for current status before assuming any study is fully done.
