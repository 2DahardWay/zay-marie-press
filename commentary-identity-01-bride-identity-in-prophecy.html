PROPHETIC IDENTITY COMMENTARY SERIES · VOLUME 1

The Bride Identity in Prophecy

The Wife of the LORD, the Wife of the Lamb, and the Marriage Pattern Paul Teaches the Body

Is the wife of the LORD in the prophets, and the Lamb’s wife in Revelation, Israel or the church?

The prophets call Israel the wife of the LORD, charge her with unfaithfulness, and promise to take her back. Revelation announces the marriage of the Lamb to his wife and shows the bride as a city whose gates carry the names of Israel’s tribes. This commentary argues that these are one wife, and defends the position from the whole Bible.

It reads the marriage in Hosea, Isaiah, Jeremiah and Ezekiel, the Bridegroom of the Gospels, Psalm 45, the marriage of the Lamb, the harlot and the bride, and the three places where Paul uses the figure of marriage. It then gives the reasons the Body of Christ is not the Bride, and answers the strongest objections.

ABOUT THIS VOLUME

A Commentary That Argues a Position

This volume does not survey the views and leave the choice to the reader. It states what the author believes the bridal passages teach, sets out the evidence from Genesis to Revelation, and answers those who disagree where they are strongest, including from modern commentaries.

It is the first volume of the Prophetic Identity Commentary Series, which asks who the people of the prophets and of Revelation are. It is a different kind of book from the Digital Studies, which walk through a passage and report what the text says. The volume gives the church’s love for Christ its full place, and asks only that the names the prophets gave to Israel be left where the prophets put them. Scripture is quoted from the King James Version throughout.
