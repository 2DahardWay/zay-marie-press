PASSAGE STUDY PACKETS

Study One Passage at a Time.

Concise, printable studies that examine one important biblical text in context, explain its doctrinal significance, and provide a clear teaching outline without expanding into a full Digital Study.

A FOCUSED STUDY FORMAT

Go deeper without losing the passage.

Each Passage Study Packet begins with the immediate literary and biblical setting, follows the passage verse by verse, traces its logic, and distinguishes explicit textual evidence from responsible inference. Meaning comes first, assignment second, and application third.

Packets are intentionally limited to one passage and one controlling interpretive question. Broader doctrines, extended biblical surveys, assessments, and guided exercises remain within the larger Digital Studies and Biblical Hermeneutics Course.

Every packet includes

  • Passage and summary line
  • Verse-by-verse exposition
  • Doctrinal significance
  • Logic chain and cross-references
  • Interpretive and apologetic notes
  • Teaching outline
  • Responsible application
  • Closing doctrinal summary
Format: 3–6 printable PDF pages
Price: $2.99–$3.99 per packet

CHOOSE THE RIGHT DEPTH

Packets and Digital Studies serve different purposes.

Passage Study Packets

One passage, one controlling question, and one concise teaching resource. Best when you need focused exposition and a usable outline without an extended topical survey.

Digital Studies

A sustained 13–17-page examination of a doctrine, biblical subject, difficult passage, or interpretive distinction, including guided study and broader biblical development.
