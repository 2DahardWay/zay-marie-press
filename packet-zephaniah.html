Bible Book Companion: Zephaniah

The Day of the LORD, the Meek Remnant, and the King in Their Midst

How does Zephaniah move from the great day of the LORD to a humble remnant and the King rejoicing in Zion, and what does it leave undisclosed?

The free Zephaniah summary orients you: setting, structure, themes, and key passages. This companion takes the next step and interprets. It reads the book’s doctrine in the King James Version and sets it in a structure you can teach from.

ABOUT THIS COMPANION

Interpretation, Not Just Orientation

A Bible Book Companion is an eleven-section guide to one book. It gives the book’s setting and program identity, follows its movements, traces its themes and its witness to Christ, separates what belongs to a program from what holds for all people, and ends with a teaching outline and usage notes.

Readers who want the whole book first can start with the free Zephaniah summary. Readers who want one theme treated in full will find it in Study 7 and Study 9. This companion follows the whole book and asks one narrower question: how does Zephaniah move from the great day of the LORD to a humble remnant and the King rejoicing in Zion?
