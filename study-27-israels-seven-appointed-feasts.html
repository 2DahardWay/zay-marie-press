Israel’s Seven Appointed Feasts

Their Covenant Setting, Historical Observance, and Prophetic Significance

A Biblical Examination of Leviticus 23, Israel’s Covenant Calendar, Christological Fulfillment, and Responsible Prophetic Inference

Leviticus 23 orders seven annual observances for Israel under the Mosaic covenant. Each feast belongs first to the nation’s historical, agricultural, priestly, and worship setting.

This study examines Passover through Tabernacles, identifies the prophetic connections Scripture states explicitly, evaluates additional proposed correspondences carefully, and explains why Israel’s ceremonial calendar does not govern the Body of Christ.

ABOUT THIS STUDY

Read the Feasts Within Israel’s Covenant Calendar

The LORD appointed the feasts to Israel, joining sacred time to national history, the land, harvest, priestly service, remembrance, cleansing, and rejoicing.

This study establishes that original setting before examining Christ our Passover, Christ the Firstfruits, Pentecost within Israel’s Prophecy Program, later prophetic associations, and the non-transfer of Israel’s calendar to the Body of Christ.

How These Studies Relate

WHAT YOU’LL STUDY

Establish the Meaning Before Drawing the Prophetic Pattern

The Appointed Times

Read Leviticus 23 as Israel’s covenant calendar and distinguish the weekly Sabbath from the seven annual observances.

Spring Observances

Examine Passover, Unleavened Bread, Firstfruits, and Weeks in their historical and agricultural settings.

Seventh-Month Feasts

Study Trumpets, the Day of Atonement, and Tabernacles with their commands, sacrifices, and national meaning.

Explicit Fulfillment

Trace the apostolic identification of Christ as our Passover and the Firstfruits of resurrection.

Prophecy and Inference

Separate explicit fulfillment from responsible inference, disputed calendar schemes, and unsupported date setting.

The Body of Christ

Explain why Pentecost belongs to Prophecy and why Israel’s feast calendar does not govern the Body.

KEY SCRIPTURES

Trace the Feasts Across Scripture

Exodus 12–13

Passover, Unleavened Bread, blood, deliverance, departure, and memorial.

Leviticus 23

The Sabbath and the complete annual cycle of Israel’s appointed observances.

Leviticus 16

The high priest, sacrificial blood, sanctuary cleansing, and the two goats.

Numbers 10; 28–29

Trumpet functions and the offerings appointed for Israel’s calendar.

Joel 2; Acts 2

Pentecost interpreted from Israel’s prophetic Scriptures and addressed to Israel.

1 Corinthians 5; 15

Christ explicitly identified as our Passover and the Firstfruits of resurrection.

Hebrews 9–10

Day-of-Atonement imagery and the superiority of Christ’s once-for-all sacrifice.

Zechariah 12–14

National mourning, cleansing, kingdom reign, and future observance of Tabernacles.

FROM THE STUDY

Meaning Comes Before Prophetic Inference

“Later typology, fulfillment, quotation, or discussion does not erase a feast’s original audience or covenant setting.”

Every proposed connection must be classified by the strength of its evidence while Israel’s covenant assignment and the Body’s completeness in Christ remain distinct.
