The Shrewd Manager

Passage: Luke 16:1–13

You will follow the manager’s impending dismissal, his calculated changes to debt records, the master’s limited commendation, and Jesus’ teaching about money, faithfulness, and competing masters. You will separate foresight from dishonesty, avoid pretending the text explains every accounting detail, and locate the disciples’ instruction beside the Pharisees’ later response. You will draw a bounded lesson about stewardship without making wealth a way to buy eternal standing or shifting the story’s original Kingdom address to the Body of Christ.
