ABOUT THIS PACKET

Interpretation, Not Just Orientation

A packet selects. It does not walk through the whole book verse by verse. It reads the passages that carry the book’s doctrine closely and summarizes the rest by structure and theme, then places the book within God’s dealings as Acts Overlap Theology reads them.

Readers who want the whole book first can start with the free Hosea summary. Readers who want one theme treated in full will find it in Study 52 and Study 12. This packet surveys the whole book and asks one narrower question: how does Hosea use a marriage sign to speak of Israel’s unfaithfulness, the judgment of exile, and the promised return?

How Packets Relate

A packet sits between a free summary and a full study. The pages below supply the neighboring ground.
