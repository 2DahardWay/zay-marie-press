The Lord’s Ministry Route Map

From Bethlehem to the Mount of Olives, Stop by Stop

The Gospels follow the Lord from His birth to His ascension. This map puts the whole route on one page: Nazareth and Capernaum, the circuits of Galilee, the feasts in Jerusalem and the final road. Tap a stop to see what happens there, who is there, and the passage in the KJV.

This guide argues a position, and says so. It reads the Lord’s ministry before the Cross as what He told and did for Israel, and it marks the places where the Gospels record a Gentile or Samaritan contact and reports them as written. Where a Gospel does not name a place, the map says its marker is a placement, not a finding. The lines between stops show the order of the visits, not the roads.

The Map

Tap a marker or a stop in the list. Use the view buttons to move from the whole land to Galilee, Judaea and Jerusalem.

Numbers are the order of the stops; a place visited more than once shows the first number and a count. Solid line: between places the text names. Dashed line: a leg that ends at a place the map has placed. Lines show order, not roads.

The Route in Order

Forty-eight stops in eight phases. The Gospels do not give one strict order; the map follows Matthew and Mark for Galilee and John for the feasts.

What the Map Does Not Claim

Where Scripture gives no date, no site or no single order, the map says so instead of filling it in.
