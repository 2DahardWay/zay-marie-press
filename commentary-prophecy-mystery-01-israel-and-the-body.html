PROPHECY & MYSTERY COMMENTARY SERIES · VOLUME 1

Israel & the Body

Two Programs, Two Identities

Israel is not the Body, and the Body is not Israel. What does each passage say, and to whom?

Scripture runs two programs. Israel’s is prophetic, national and earthly; the Body’s is the Mystery revealed to Paul, with a heavenly calling. This commentary argues that the two ran side by side through Acts 9–28 and never merged, that Acts 28 reports a national suspension and not a replacement, and that neither program’s identity, covenants or hope passes to the other.

It reads Israel’s program, the Body’s program, their separate beginnings and the years of the overlap, then takes up the passages most often used to join them. In Chapter 9 it sets its reading beside eight modern writers, four who stand with it and four who read the matter differently, and answers each in his own words.

ABOUT THIS VOLUME

A Commentary That Argues a Position

This volume does not survey the readings of Israel and the Body and leave the choice to the reader. It states what the author believes the passages teach, sets out the evidence from the prophets, the Gospels and Acts to the letters of Paul, and answers the strongest objections in the objectors’ own terms, naming the modern writers who hold them.

It is the first volume of the Prophecy & Mystery Commentary Series. It is a different kind of book from the Digital Studies, which walk through a passage and report what the text says. Scripture is quoted from the King James Version throughout, and the writers named in Chapter 9 are quoted only from their own published words.
