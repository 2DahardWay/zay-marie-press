TYPOLOGY COMMENTARY SERIES · VOLUME 2

Prophetic Separation

The Pattern of Separating the Judged from the Kept, and Where “One Taken, One Left” Stands in It

Who is taken, who is left, and whose scene is it?

Noah, Lot and Israel in Goshen each show God drawing a line before judgment falls. This commentary reads those scenes in order, then turns to the Lord’s words in Matthew 24 and Luke 17 and asks what He says, to whom, and what He leaves unsaid.

It walks the wheat and the tares, the net, the virgins and the sheep and the goats, follows Israel’s sifting in the prophets and the harvests of the Revelation, and sets the Body’s removal in its own place. In Chapter 12 it sets its reading beside modern writers who see it differently.

ABOUT THIS VOLUME

A Commentary That Argues a Position

This volume does not survey what has been taught about the one taken and the one left and leave the choice to the reader. It states what the author believes the passages teach, sets out the evidence from the Law, the Prophets, the Gospels, the letters and the Revelation, and answers the strongest objections, naming the modern writers who hold them.

It is the second volume of the Typology Commentary Series. It is a different kind of book from the Digital Studies, which walk through passages and report what the text says. Scripture is quoted from the King James Version throughout, and the writers named in Chapter 12 are quoted only from words the author has read in full.
