The Beginning of the Body of Christ

Why the Church Which Is Christ’s Body Begins with Paul at Acts 9

A Biblical Examination of Pentecost, Ekklesia, Paul’s Calling, the Mystery Revealed Through Paul, and the Acts Overlap

The question is not merely when a believing assembly first existed. Scripture must establish when the Church identified by Paul as Christ’s Body entered history.

This study tests Pentecost in its own prophetic setting, distinguishes ekklesia from program identity, follows Paul’s calling at Acts 9, and explains why the Body begins with Paul while Israel’s Prophecy Program continues distinctly through the Acts Overlap.

ABOUT THIS STUDY

When Did the Body of Christ Begin?

The Church, which is Christ’s Body, belongs to the previously hidden Mystery revealed through Paul. Because the Body is part of that Mystery Program, it cannot be placed historically within Israel’s Prophecy Program before the Mystery begins with Paul in Acts 9.

Pentecost remains fully within Israel’s prophetic setting—Joel’s prophecy, Israel’s promises, Davidic expectation, and the coming Kingdom. Acts 9 marks the beginning of the Mystery Program without terminating Prophecy; both operate distinctly and concurrently until Acts 28, where Prophecy is suspended and Mystery continues.
