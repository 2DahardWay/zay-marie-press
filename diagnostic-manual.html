THE DIAGNOSTIC PROTOCOL

Seven Questions for Every Acts Passage

The protocol begins with diagnosis before application, guiding the reader to identify the passage’s audience, program, chronology, doctrinal markers, and warranted application.

01

Who Is Speaking?

Identify the principal speaker or actor. Is the passage centered on Peter, the Twelve, another Prophecy-program figure, or Paul?

02

Who Is the Audience?

Determine whether the passage addresses Israel, a Jewish audience, Gentiles, a mixed audience, or those associated with Paul’s ministry.

03

What Divine Program Governs the Passage?

Identify whether the passage belongs to Israel’s Prophecy Program, Paul’s Mystery Program for the Body of Christ, or the historical Acts 9–28 setting in which both programs are operating concurrently.

04

What Covenant or Revelatory Basis Is Present?

Is the passage grounded in Israel’s prophetic covenants and promises, or in the Mystery revealed through Paul?

05

What Signs, Ordinances, or Program Markers Appear?

Note elements such as signs, miracles, water baptism, synagogue ministry, covenant appeal, or distinctly Pauline Mystery doctrine.

06

Where Does the Passage Fall in the Acts Chronology?

Is it before Acts 9, within the Acts 9–28 overlap, at Acts 28, or beyond Acts 28?

07

What Doctrinal Application Is Warranted?

After identifying audience, program, chronology, and doctrinal markers, determine what the passage teaches, what belongs to its historical/program context, and what may properly be applied to the Body of Christ.

The protocol is reinforced through passage classification, comparison tables, diagnostic questions, application exercises, error detection, and teaching tools.

THE ACTS 9–28 OVERLAP

The Chronology Behind the Diagnosis

Every passage in Acts must be understood within its proper historical and doctrinal setting. The diagnostic method of The Diagnostic Manual therefore pays careful attention not only to what a passage says, but also to where it stands within the Acts chronology.

PROPHECYBegins EarlierIsrael's prophetic program
→
ACTS 9Mystery BeginsPaul's calling and commission
→
ACTS 9–28Concurrent OverlapTwo distinct programs operate side-by-side
→
ACTS 28Prophecy SuspendedIsrael officially set aside
→
BEYOND ACTS 28Mystery ContinuesUntil the fullness of the Gentiles

During Acts 9–28, God’s Prophecy Program for Israel and Mystery Program for the Body of Christ operate concurrently, each retaining its own audience, message, and divine purpose. Recognizing this overlap prevents the interpreter from collapsing the two programs into one or treating Acts as though a single doctrinal administration governs every passage.

At Acts 28, Israel’s Prophecy Program reaches its suspension point, while the Mystery Program continues toward the fullness of the Gentiles.
