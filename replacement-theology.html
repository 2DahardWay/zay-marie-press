WHY THIS GUIDE MATTERS

Concurrency Is Not Transfer

Replacement Theology depends on reading a promise addressed to Israel as though the Church was its true recipient all along. This guide tests that move directly, at survey depth, while pointing to the full biblical case wherever one already exists.

Grounded in the Framework

Built on the Acts Overlap Theology framework's governing Non-Transfer Principle: historical concurrency between Israel's Prophecy Program and the Body's Mystery Program does not create doctrinal transfer.

Survey Depth, Not a Substitute

Each replacement claim is answered briefly, then pointed to the Digital Study that develops the full biblical argument passage by passage.

Paired with Historical Drift

Historical Drift traces how the error developed. This guide picks up where it leaves off and tests whether the resulting claims are true.
