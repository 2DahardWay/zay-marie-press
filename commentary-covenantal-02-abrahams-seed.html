Abraham’s Seed

Who the Seed Is, and Who It Is Not

When God spoke of Abraham’s seed, whom did he mean?

“Seed” is one of the most used words in the promises to Abraham, and it does not always name the same persons. This commentary argues that in Genesis it names a chosen line, through Isaac and Jacob to Israel and to the house of David, and that the line comes to its head in Christ, the one Seed to whom the promise was made. It argues that the Body of Christ is counted as Abraham’s seed only because it belongs to Christ, receives blessing through Christ alone, and is not made Israel.

It follows the word from Genesis through the psalms and the prophets to Paul in Galatians and Romans, and it reads the passages most often used to make the Body Israel. In Chapter 9 it sets its reading beside seven modern writers, three who stand with it and four who read the promises differently, and answers each in his own words.

ABOUT THIS VOLUME

A Commentary That Argues a Position

This volume does not survey the readings of Abraham’s seed and leave the choice to the reader. It states what the author believes the passages teach, sets out the evidence from Genesis through the prophets to the letters of Paul, and answers the strongest objections in the objectors’ own terms, naming the modern writers who hold them.

It is the second volume of the Covenantal Commentary Series. The first asked what God promised Abraham and who the promise belongs to; this one asks who the seed is. It is a different kind of book from the Digital Studies, which walk through a passage and report what the text says. Scripture is quoted from the King James Version throughout, and the writers named in Chapter 9 are quoted only from their own published words.

KEY SCRIPTURES

Read the Word in Its Context

Genesis 21:9–13

“In Isaac shall thy seed be called”: the line chosen, and Ishmael’s place beside it.

Isaiah 41:8–10

Israel, “the seed of Abraham my friend,” comforted in exile.

Jeremiah 31:35–37

The seed of Israel cannot cease while the sun and moon stand.

2 Samuel 7:12–16

The seed of David, and the throne established for ever.

Galatians 3:16–19

“He saith not, And to seeds … but as of one, … which is Christ.”

Galatians 3:26–29

Christ’s, and so Abraham’s seed and heirs according to the promise.

Romans 4:9–17

Abraham the father of all who believe, circumcised or not.

Romans 9:4–8

The children of the promise are counted for the seed.
