BIBLE BOOK COMPANION SERIES

Bible Book Companion Series

Interpretation you can trust, one book at a time.

Each Bible Book Companion is a concise, printable doctrinal guide to one biblical book. It identifies the book’s Program‑Specific Truth (Israel’s Prophecy program or the Body of Christ’s Mystery program) and its Universal Truth, maps how the book fits into the Prophecy–Mystery storyline, and provides charts and a teaching outline for confident, accurate study. Each Companion keeps the book in its proper program so you can read it clearly, without blending Israel’s covenants with the Mystery revealed to Paul.

All 66 books have a Bible Book Companion, each priced at $2.99. Checkout coming soon.
