DOCTRINAL PACKETS

Doctrinal Packets

One book at a time, interpreted and ready to teach

A Doctrinal Packet is a short, printable interpretive guide to one book of the Bible. It reads the passages that carry the book’s doctrine in the King James Version, places the book within God’s programs, and gives you charts and a teaching outline. Packets are new; more will follow.

HOW THE PIECES FIT

Summary, Packet, Study

Each piece has its own job. Use whichever fits your question.

PieceWhat it doesLength and price
Bible Book SummaryOrients you to a whole book: setting, structure, themes, key passages. Descriptive.Free
