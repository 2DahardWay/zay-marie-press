ACTS OVERLAP THEOLOGY SERIES

Two Distinct Streams. One Overlap.

THE SERIES

Distinct. Concurrent. Divinely Coordinated.

The Acts Overlap Theology Series examines the Book of Acts through the distinction between God's Prophecy Program for Israel and His Mystery Program for the Body of Christ. Beginning with Paul's calling in Acts 9, the two programs operate concurrently through Acts 28—distinct in audience, message, and divine purpose, yet unfolding side-by-side within the same historical period.

What Is the Acts Overlap?

The Acts Overlap is the period from Acts 9 through Acts 28 during which God's Prophecy Program for Israel and His Mystery Program for the Body of Christ operate concurrently. Israel's prophetic program remains active during this period while the Mystery Program, revealed through Paul, is simultaneously being made known.

At Acts 28, Israel's Prophecy Program reaches its suspension point, while the Mystery Program continues toward the fullness of the Gentiles.
