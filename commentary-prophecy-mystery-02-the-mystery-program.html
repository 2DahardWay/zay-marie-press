PROPHECY & MYSTERY COMMENTARY SERIES · VOLUME 2

The Mystery Program

Hid in God, Revealed Through Paul

What is the Mystery, what did God reveal in it, to whom, and when?

Paul names a secret that was kept in God before the world began and not made known to the sons of men in other ages. This commentary argues that the secret was revealed to Paul, that its content is Jew and Gentile one body in Christ with a heavenly seat, that it was committed to him as a stewardship, and that it began at Damascus, ran beside Israel’s program through Acts 9–28 and goes on beyond the Acts.

It reads every place the word appears, because the word does not always name this secret, and it tests the claim that the prophets foretold it. In Chapter 9 it sets its reading beside seven modern writers, three who stand with it and four who read the matter differently, and answers each in his own words.

ABOUT THIS VOLUME

A Commentary That Argues a Position

This volume does not survey the readings of the mystery and leave the choice to the reader. It states what the author believes the passages teach, sets out the evidence from Paul’s letters, the Acts and the prophets, and answers the strongest objections in the objectors’ own terms, naming the modern writers who hold them.

It is the second volume of the Prophecy & Mystery Commentary Series. It is a different kind of book from the Digital Studies, which walk through a passage and report what the text says. Scripture is quoted from the King James Version throughout, and the writers named in Chapter 9 are quoted only from their own published words.
