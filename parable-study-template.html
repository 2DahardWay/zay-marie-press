Parable Study Template

A guided worksheet for reading any parable in its Gospel setting

Eight short sections walk you through who Jesus is addressing, what He is answering, the comparison He supplies, what He names and what He leaves unnamed, where the parable stops, and what it says. Print it, fill it in, and keep it with your Bible.

Free to every reader of the Parables studies. A worked example on the Wedding Banquet shows the template filled in.

The eight sections

  1. Passage Information
  2. Audience & Occasion
  3. The Comparison Jesus Supplies
  4. What Jesus Names vs. What He Leaves Unnamed
  5. Boundaries of the Parable
  6. The Stated Point
  7. Present Lesson (Only When the Parable Supplies One)
  8. Notes & Observations

How to use them

  1. Choose a parable and read its Gospel passage through once in the KJV.
  2. Work through the template one section at a time, writing only what the text states.
  3. Compare your page with the worked example, then read the Parable Study for that parable to check your reading against a full study.
