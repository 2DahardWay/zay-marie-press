COVENANTAL COMMENTARY SERIES · VOLUME 1

The Abrahamic Covenant

What God Swore to Abraham, and to Whom

What did God promise Abraham, and who does the promise belong to?

God made one covenant with Abraham, cut it while Abraham slept, and confirmed it with an oath. This commentary argues that the covenant is unconditional, that its terms of land, nation, name and blessing were repeated to Isaac, to Jacob and to Israel, and that Sinai neither added to it nor took it away. It argues that the Body of Christ receives blessing through Christ alone and does not take the place of the people to whom the promises were made.

It follows the covenant from the call in Genesis 12 through the covenant ceremony of Genesis 15, the sign of Genesis 17 and the oath of Genesis 22, then through Israel’s own Scriptures to Paul in Galatians, Romans and Ephesians. In Chapter 9 it sets its reading beside seven modern writers, three who stand with it and four who read the promises differently, and answers each in his own words.

ABOUT THIS VOLUME

A Commentary That Argues a Position

This volume does not survey the readings of the Abrahamic covenant and leave the choice to the reader. It states what the author believes the passages teach, sets out the evidence from Genesis through the prophets to the letters of Paul, and answers the strongest objections in the objectors’ own terms, naming the modern writers who hold them.

It is the first volume of the Covenantal Commentary Series, which examines the covenant made with Abraham and the themes that flow from it. It is a different kind of book from the Digital Studies, which walk through a passage and report what the text says. Scripture is quoted from the King James Version throughout, and the writers named in Chapter 9 are quoted only from their own published words.

KEY SCRIPTURES

Read the Word in Its Context

Genesis 12:1–7

The call, the promise of a great nation and a blessing, and the land shown to Abram’s seed.

Genesis 15:1–21

The covenant cut: a deep sleep, a smoking furnace and a burning lamp, and the boundaries of the land.

Genesis 17:1–21

The everlasting covenant, the sign of circumcision and the covenant established with Isaac.

Genesis 22:15–18

The oath sworn by the LORD himself, and the blessing of all nations.

Leviticus 26:40–45

Remembered in the land of the enemies: the covenant with Jacob, Isaac and Abraham.

Galatians 3:15–22

A covenant confirmed before of God in Christ, and a law that cannot disannul it.

Romans 4:9–25

Abraham, the father of all who believe, and the promise that he should be heir of the world.

Hebrews 6:13–20

Two immutable things, the promise and the oath, in which it was impossible for God to lie.
