ABOUT THIS PACKET

Interpretation, Not Just Orientation

A packet selects. It does not walk through the whole book verse by verse. It reads the passages that carry the book’s doctrine closely and summarizes the rest by structure and theme, then places the book within God’s dealings as Acts Overlap Theology reads them.

Readers who want the whole book first can start with the free Proverbs summary. Readers who want one theme treated in full will find it in Study 39 and Study 59. This packet surveys the whole book and asks one narrower question: how does Proverbs set wisdom against folly, and what does it teach about the fear of the LORD?

Selective Exegesis: Proverbs 22:17–24:34 — Words of the wise

Remove not the ancient landmark, which thy fathers have set.PROVERBS 22:28 · KJV
  • The voice changes to direct address: incline your ear and hear the words of the wise (22:17).
  • Some sayings guard the poor and the boundaries of the land: do not rob the poor (22:22–23) and do not move the ancient landmark (22:28; 23:10; compare Deut. 19:14).
  • It warns against envying the wicked (23:17; 24:1, 19) and against drunkenness and gluttony (23:19–21, 29–35), and it ends with the sluggard’s field (24:30–34). A second heading marks more sayings of the wise (24:23).

The packet gives six passages this treatment, each tied to the verses it reads.
