How Can We Help?

Answers and practical guidance for Zay-Marie Press readers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I purchase Zay-Marie Press books?

Zay-Marie Press books will be available through the Books section of this website as titles are released. Purchase information and available formats will be provided with each title.

What are Zay-Marie Press Digital Studies?

Digital Studies are focused, Scripture-centered studies designed for convenient personal study and reference. They address specific biblical and theological subjects in a concise format while maintaining the doctrinal clarity and careful structure of Zay-Marie Press publications.

How will I receive a Digital Study after purchase?

Digital Studies will be delivered electronically. Complete download and access instructions will be provided when digital purchasing launches.

May I print a Digital Study for my own use?

Yes. Purchased Digital Studies may be printed for the purchaser's personal study and reference, subject to the usage terms provided with each publication. They may not be reproduced, redistributed, resold, or shared as substitute copies for others.

Does Zay-Marie Press offer free biblical resources?

Yes. The Resources section provides selected biblical materials for readers who want to explore Scripture and become familiar with the teaching approach of Zay-Marie Press.

What is the Acts Overlap Theology Series?

The Acts Overlap Theology Series examines the Book of Acts through the distinction between God's Prophecy Program for Israel and His Mystery Program for the Body of Christ, including their concurrent operation during the Acts 9–28 overlap.

How can I learn about upcoming books and studies?

New and forthcoming publications will be announced throughout the Zay-Marie Press website. As the publishing program expands, additional ways to receive publication updates may also be provided.

What should I do if I need help with a purchase, download, or other question?

Visit Reader Support for shipping and returns information, digital download guidance, and other assistance. For questions requiring individual help, use Contact Support to reach Zay-Marie Press.

Shipping & Returns

Physical Book Orders

Zay-Marie Press books may be made available through multiple booksellers and distribution channels. Shipping options, delivery estimates, charges, and order processing will depend on the retailer or fulfillment provider through which a purchase is made.

Shipping Information

For books purchased through an outside retailer, the retailer's shipping terms, delivery estimates, and tracking procedures will apply. Readers should consult the order confirmation or retailer account for the most current shipping information.

Returns & Refunds

Return and refund policies for physical books are generally determined by the retailer or fulfillment provider from which the book was purchased. Readers should follow that seller's return procedures when requesting a return, replacement, or refund.

Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Books

If a physical book arrives damaged, contains a manufacturing defect, or is not the title or edition ordered, readers should first contact the retailer or fulfillment provider that processed the order. Their customer-service team will normally handle replacement or refund requests associated with fulfillment.

Orders Purchased Directly from Zay-Marie Press

If Zay-Marie Press offers direct physical-book purchasing in the future, the applicable shipping, return, refund, and replacement policies will be clearly provided before purchase and published on this page.

Need Help?

If you are unsure where to direct an order-related question, contact Zay-Marie Press through Contact Support. We will help identify the appropriate next step whenever possible.

Digital Downloads

Accessing Your Digital Studies

Zay-Marie Press Digital Studies are delivered electronically as PDF files. When digital purchasing launches, customers will receive download instructions as part of the purchase process. Follow the instructions provided with your order to access your files.

Saving Your Files

After downloading a Digital Study, save the PDF to a secure location on your computer, tablet, or other supported device. We recommend keeping a personal backup so the study remains available for future reference.

Printing for Personal Use

Purchased Digital Studies may be printed for the purchaser's personal study and reference. Unless expressly stated otherwise, digital publications may not be reproduced, redistributed, resold, uploaded for public access, or shared as substitute copies for other individuals.

Using Digital Studies on Your Devices

PDF files can generally be opened with standard PDF-reading software on computers, tablets, and compatible mobile devices. The appearance and available features may vary depending on the device and PDF reader being used.

Problems Accessing a Download

If you complete a purchase but are unable to access or download your files, first review the download instructions provided with your order. If the problem continues, contact Zay-Marie Press through Contact Support and include the email address used for the purchase and your order number, when available.

Lost or Deleted Files

If a purchased Digital Study is accidentally deleted or lost, contact Contact Support. When possible, we will help determine whether access can be restored through the applicable digital-delivery provider. Availability of replacement downloads may depend on the platform through which the original purchase was made.

Digital Purchases and Refunds

Because Digital Studies are downloadable products, the applicable refund policy will be presented as part of the purchasing process when digital sales launch. Any platform-specific terms governing a purchase will also apply.

Need Help?

For assistance with accessing, downloading, saving, or using a Zay-Marie Press Digital Study, visit Contact Support.

Contact Support

How Can We Help?

Zay-Marie Press welcomes questions concerning our publications, Digital Studies, website resources, purchases, and reader support. Before contacting us, you may find an immediate answer in the FAQ, Shipping & Returns, or Digital Downloads sections above.

For Order-Related Questions

When contacting us about an order, please include the following information when available:

  • Your name and email address used for the purchase
  • Order number
  • Book, Digital Study, or collection involved
  • Retailer or purchasing platform, if applicable
  • A brief description of the issue

Please do not send credit-card numbers, passwords, or other sensitive payment information.

For Digital Download Assistance

If you are having difficulty accessing a Digital Study, tell us which study or collection you purchased, the email address used for the purchase, your order number when available, and what happens when you attempt to access the file.

For General Questions

Questions concerning Zay-Marie Press, our publications, the Acts Overlap Theology Series, free resources, or other website content are also welcome.

Contact Zay-Marie Press

Email: info@zaymariepress.com

We will review your inquiry and respond as soon as reasonably possible.

Manuscript Submissions
