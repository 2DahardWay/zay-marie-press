Prophetic Commentary Series

One theme in each volume, argued from the whole Bible

Each volume takes a single prophetic question, states a position and defends it from Genesis to Revelation. Opposing views are quoted in their own words, with their sources named, and answered from Scripture.

THE SERIES

What Every Volume Does

One Theme

Each volume takes one question and gives it room, in about fifty to seventy pages.

A Stated Position

The volume says what it holds and why, and does not leave the reader to sort out the views alone.

Fair to Opponents

Those who disagree are quoted accurately, with the work and the source given, and never made to say more than they said.

King James Text

Scripture is quoted from the King James Version, and every quotation is checked against it.

A Bibliography

Every source is listed, with where it can be checked.

Digital PDF

Each volume is priced from $14.99 to $19.99 and licensed for personal use.

The Prophetic Commentary Series is separate from the Digital Studies. A Digital Study walks through a passage and reports what the text says; a Commentary Volume argues a position on one theme.
