The New Covenant

Israel’s Promised Restoration Under Messiah

A Biblical Examination of Israel and Judah, Covenant Promises, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, Christ’s Finished Work, the Acts Overlap, and Kingdom Restoration

The New Covenant is announced to the house of Israel and the house of Judah. This study follows its covenant parties, promises, redemptive basis, and future fulfillment from Jeremiah and Ezekiel through Christ’s finished work, Romans 11, and Israel’s Kingdom restoration.

It shows why shared redemption in Christ does not create shared covenant identity: the Body of Christ’s blessings belong to the Mystery revealed through Paul, while the New Covenant remains Israel’s promised restoration.

ABOUT THIS STUDY

What Is the New Covenant?

Jeremiah 31 identifies Israel and Judah as the New Covenant’s named parties. Ezekiel 36 complements that promise with cleansing, a new heart, the Spirit within restored Israel, regathering, and restoration to the land. The covenant arrangement is new; the named covenant people are not.

Christ’s finished work provides the redemptive basis for the covenant without changing its recipients. During the Acts Overlap, Israel’s covenant promises remain Israel’s, even while the Mystery Program concerning the Body of Christ operates distinctly and concurrently.

This is not a study of how the Church may learn from Scripture written to Israel, which Study 39 works out as a general method, nor of the broad Israel–Body distinction itself, which Study 4 establishes across every point of contrast. Its task is narrower: to read Jeremiah 31 and Ezekiel 36 on their own terms, and to ask whether Christ’s finished redemptive work changes the covenant’s named recipients or only supplies its basis.

How These Studies Relate

Study 12 identifies the New Covenant’s named recipients and promises in Jeremiah 31 and Ezekiel 36.

Study 39 — The Church’s Relationship to Israel’s Scriptures Work through how the Body may learn from a covenant addressed to Israel and Judah without acquiring its national identity or every covenant obligation.

Study 4 — Israel and the Body of Christ Place the covenant within the wider distinction between Israel and the Body of Christ; this clarifies why Christ’s saving work and the covenant’s named parties are separate questions.

FROM THE STUDY

The Covenant Arrangement Is New; Its People Are Not

“Christ’s finished work provides the New Covenant’s redemptive basis. It does not change the covenant parties named by Jeremiah.”

Receiving blessing through Christ does not automatically make a person or company a party to Israel’s covenant. The covenant belongs to Israel’s Prophecy Program; the Body’s blessings belong to the Mystery revealed through Paul.
