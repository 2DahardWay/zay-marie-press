The Wise and Foolish Builders

Passage: Matthew 7:24–27; Luke 6:47–49

A disciplined study of two builders, a storm that strikes both houses, and one house that stands while the other falls. This edition reads the parable as the close of Jesus’ teaching in Matthew 5–7 and Luke 6, marking the one Jesus likens to each builder and what He leaves unnamed. It sets Matthew’s rock and sand beside Luke’s dug foundation and flood, and keeps the account distinct from the neighboring Rich Fool without assigning identities Jesus does not give.

About this study

This study follows the two builders in the order Jesus tells them, from hearing to building to the storm to the house that stands or falls. It records the one Jesus likens to each builder and marks every part He leaves unnamed — the rock, the sand, the house, and the storm. The study compares Matthew’s account with Luke’s, including the question Luke places before the parable, and keeps both separate from the Rich Fool. It states a principled present lesson without transferring the Kingdom address to the Body of Christ. Related studies share the theme of hearing and doing, but each edition keeps its own passage, movement, and interpretive boundaries.
