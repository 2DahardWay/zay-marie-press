ACTS OVERLAP THEOLOGY SERIES — BOOK 1

THE FULL FRAMEWORK

The Complete Integrated Doctrine of the Acts Overlap Model

Alexander S. Marien

Readers of Acts encounter a structural tension that few can put into words. Peter and the Twelve proclaim Israel’s kingdom to Israel, while Paul is sent to the Gentiles with the gospel of the grace of God—and both ministries operate side by side in the same book. Most Bible students sense this shift yet struggle to trace it consistently from chapter to chapter. The Full Framework offers one clear model: from Acts 9 through Acts 28, two distinct programs of God run concurrently, and this volume shows you how to distinguish them passage by passage.

The Full Framework, Book 1 of the Acts Overlap Theology Series, establishes the complete doctrinal architecture of the Acts Overlap Model. It defines the Prophecy and Mystery Programs, traces their concurrent operation from Acts 9–28, and sets the structural boundaries that govern every later volume. Across twenty-one chapters and Appendices A–T, the book lays out the system’s categories, interpretive rules, diagrams, timelines, and diagnostic lenses, forming the canonical reference for pastors, teachers, students, and serious readers who need a precise, program-specific understanding of Acts.

Written for pastors, teachers, and serious Bible students who want a consistent way to read Acts 9–28 and to understand the relationship between Prophecy and Mystery.

392 pages · 6 × 9 in · 21 chapters · Appendices A–T with charts, glossary, worksheets and timelines

What You’ll Learn

  • Why Acts 9–28 records two concurrent programs of God, not one program changing course
  • How Paul’s commission and gospel remain distinct from the ministry of the Twelve
  • Why Acts 28 marks the national suspension of Israel’s Prophecy Program
  • How to identify whether a passage belongs to the Prophecy Program or the Mystery Program
  • How the Acts Overlap Model addresses recurring interpretive tensions, with charts, a glossary, worksheets and timelines to apply it

THE ACTS 9–28 OVERLAP

Two Programs. One Historical Overlap.

The Mystery Program begins at Acts 9; it does not emerge from Israel’s Prophecy Program. Both programs then operate concurrently through Acts 28.

At Acts 28, Israel’s Prophecy Program reaches its national suspension, while the Mystery Program continues toward the fullness of the Gentiles.

  1. ProphecyBegins EarlierIsrael’s prophetic program
  2. Acts 9Mystery BeginsPaul’s calling and commission
  3. Acts 9–28Concurrent OverlapDistinct programs operate side-by-side
  4. Acts 28Prophecy SuspendedIsrael’s national program suspended
