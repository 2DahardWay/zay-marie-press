THE ACTS OVERLAP THEOLOGY SERIES

Distinct. Concurrent. Divinely Coordinated.

The Acts Overlap Theology Series examines the Book of Acts through the distinction between God's Prophecy Program for Israel and His Mystery Program for the Body of Christ. Beginning with Paul's calling in Acts 9, the two programs operate concurrently through Acts 28—distinct in audience, message, and divine purpose, yet unfolding side-by-side within the same historical period.

Looking for the whole-Bible doctrinal arguments? Browse the Commentary Collection — forty volumes in eight series.

THE ACTS 9–28 OVERLAP

Two Programs. One Historical Overlap.

The Mystery Program begins at Acts 9; it does not emerge from Israel's Prophecy Program. Both programs then operate concurrently through Acts 28.

At Acts 28, Israel's Prophecy Program reaches its national suspension, while the Mystery Program continues toward the fullness of the Gentiles.
