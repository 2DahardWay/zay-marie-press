ACTS OVERLAP THEOLOGY SERIES — BOOK 2

THE DIAGNOSTIC MANUAL

The Analytical Guide to the Acts Overlap Model

Alexander S. Marien

The Diagnostic Manual advances the Acts Overlap Theology framework established in The Full Framework by providing the analytical tools needed to apply it with precision.

Through structured charts, doctrinal mapping, diagnostic tools, and exegetical analysis, this volume equips readers to identify, classify, and understand the concurrent operation of God's Prophecy Program for Israel and Mystery Program for the Body of Christ during the Acts 9–28 overlap.

Designed for pastors, teachers, students, and serious Bible readers, The Diagnostic Manual helps readers rightly divide the Word of Truth with greater precision and confidence.
