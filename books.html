ACTS OVERLAP THEOLOGY SERIES — BOOK 1

THE FULL FRAMEWORK

The Complete Integrated Doctrine of the Acts Overlap Model

Alexander S. Marien

The Full Framework, Book 1 of the Acts Overlap Theology Series, establishes the complete doctrinal architecture of the Acts Overlap Model. It defines the Prophecy and Mystery Programs, traces their concurrent operation from Acts 9–28, and sets the structural boundaries that govern every later volume. Across twenty-one chapters and Appendices A–U, the book lays out the system’s categories, interpretive rules, diagrams, timelines, and diagnostic lenses, forming the canonical reference for pastors, teachers, students, and serious readers who need a precise, program-specific understanding of Acts.

236 pages · 6 × 9 in · 21 chapters · Appendices A–U with charts, glossary, worksheets and timelines

THE ACTS 9–28 OVERLAP

Two Programs. One Historical Overlap.

The Mystery Program begins at Acts 9; it does not emerge from Israel's Prophecy Program. Both programs then operate concurrently through Acts 28.

At Acts 28, Israel's Prophecy Program reaches its national suspension, while the Mystery Program continues toward the fullness of the Gentiles.

  1. PROPHECYBegins EarlierIsrael's prophetic program
  2. ACTS 9Mystery BeginsPaul's calling and commission
  3. ACTS 9–28Concurrent OverlapDistinct programs operate side-by-side
  4. ACTS 28Prophecy SuspendedIsrael's national program suspended
  5. BEYOND ACTS 28Mystery ContinuesUntil the fullness of the Gentiles
