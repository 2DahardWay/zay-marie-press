FOUNDATIONAL VOLUME

What This Volume Establishes

The Full Framework establishes the theological architecture that governs the Acts Overlap Model and the volumes that follow. It defines the distinctions, chronological boundaries, and interpretive principles necessary for reading Acts without collapsing God's Prophecy Program for Israel into His Mystery Program for the Body of Christ.

FOUNDATION 01

Two Divine Programs

Prophecy and Mystery retain distinct audiences, messages, callings, promises, and divine purposes.

FOUNDATION 02

Chronological Boundaries

The Prophecy Program is already in operation before Acts 9. The Mystery Program begins with Paul's calling and commission in Acts 9. Both operate concurrently through Acts 28, where Israel's Prophecy Program is suspended while the Mystery Program continues.

FOUNDATION 03

Interpretive Distinctions

Audience, message, program, calling, signs, promises, and doctrinal context must be identified before application is made.

FOUNDATION 04

Concurrent Operation

Acts 9–28 records two distinct divine programs operating side-by-side—neither becoming the other, yet divinely coordinated within the same historical period.

THE GOVERNING DISTINCTION

The Two Divine Programs

The Acts Overlap Model recognizes two distinct divine programs whose concurrent operation during Acts must be understood without collapsing their respective identities and purposes.

PROPHECY

God's Program for Israel

  • Revealed since the world began
  • Earthly and national in scope
  • Covenant-based
  • Kingdom promises centered in Israel and Jerusalem
  • Already operating before Acts 9
  • Continues during the Acts overlap
  • Suspended at Acts 28
ACTS 9–28

The Concurrent Overlap

Two distinct divine programs operate within the same historical period while retaining their respective audiences, messages, callings, promises, and purposes.

DISTINCT. CONCURRENT. DIVINELY COORDINATED.

MYSTERY

God's Program for the Body of Christ

  • Hidden since the world began
  • Revealed through Paul
  • Heavenly calling
  • Not covenant-based
  • Begins with Paul's calling and commission in Acts 9
  • Operates concurrently with Prophecy through Acts 28
  • Continues beyond Acts 28
