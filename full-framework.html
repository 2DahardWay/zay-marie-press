ABOUT THE BOOK

A Framework for Reading Acts Without Collapsing Its Distinctions

The Full Framework presents the doctrinal architecture of the Acts Overlap Model and establishes the interpretive categories that govern the series. It approaches Acts as the divinely appointed period in which God's Prophecy Program for Israel and His Mystery Program for the Body of Christ operate concurrently while remaining distinct in audience, message, calling, and divine purpose.

The book gives particular attention to Acts 9–28: the Mystery Program begins with Paul's calling and commission in Acts 9 while Israel's Prophecy Program continues. The two programs therefore operate side-by-side during the overlap rather than one program transitioning into, morphing into, or replacing the other.

By establishing these boundaries first, the volume provides the reader with a consistent framework for interpreting the events, ministries, messages, and audiences encountered throughout Acts.

INSIDE THE FULL FRAMEWORK

Major Areas of Study

Prophecy and Israel

Israel's earthly, covenant-based prophetic program, its kingdom promises, and its continuing operation during Acts.

Mystery and the Body of Christ

The heavenly program revealed through Paul, the calling of the Body, and the distinct character of the Mystery revelation.

Acts 9–28 Overlap

The concurrent operation of two distinct divine programs—side-by-side and divinely coordinated during the Acts period.

Paul's Distinctive Apostleship

Paul's calling, commission, ministry, and role in making known the Mystery Program.

Israel During Acts

Israel's continued prophetic standing, the developing pattern of rejection, and the significance of the Acts 28 pronouncement.

Diagnostic Method

A disciplined way to identify audience, message, program, signs, promises, and doctrinal context without flattening biblical distinctions.
