PROPHETIC COMMENTARY SERIES · VOLUME 2

The Beast and the False Prophet

The Counterfeit Trinity, from Its Rise to the Lake of Fire

Who is the second beast of Revelation 13, and what does he do for the first?

Revelation shows three figures standing together against God and his Christ: the dragon, the beast and the false prophet. This commentary argues who the third figure is, where he comes from, and how his work fits that of the other two, and defends the position from the whole Bible.

It reads Revelation 13:11–18 verse by verse, traces the false prophet through Israel’s Scriptures, sets 2 Thessalonians 2 beside Revelation, follows the beast’s turn on Babylon to the lake of fire, and answers the strongest objections in the objectors’ own words.

ABOUT THIS VOLUME

A Commentary That Argues a Position

This volume does not survey the views and leave the choice to the reader. It states what the author believes the passages teach, sets out the evidence from Genesis to Revelation, and quotes those who disagree, with their works and sources given, so that the objections are met where they are strongest.

It is the second volume of the series and takes up where the first leaves off. Volume 1 asked who the beast is; this volume asks who stands beside him and how the two end. It is a different kind of book from the Digital Studies. Readers of Study 74 (The Man of Sin) and Study 61 (Revelation 12) will find neighbouring ground here, argued rather than only reported. Scripture is quoted from the King James Version throughout.
