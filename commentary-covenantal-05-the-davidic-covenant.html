COVENANTAL COMMENTARY SERIES · VOLUME 5

The Davidic Covenant

The Throne and the Kingdom

God swore David a house, a throne and a kingdom for ever. Where is that throne, and who sits on it?

God promised David a son whose throne and kingdom would have no end, and confirmed it by oath. This commentary argues that the promise stands: the son is Jesus, the throne is Israel’s and is on the earth, and it is taken up in the Kingdom the prophets describe. Christ now sits at the right hand of God, and the Body’s seat is with him in the heavenly places. The commentary argues that these are different seats, and that no passage moves one to another.

It follows the covenant from 2 Samuel 7 through the psalms and the prophets, the Gospels and Acts, Hebrews and Revelation, and it reads the passages most often used to place Jesus on David’s throne now. In Chapter 9 it sets its reading beside five modern writers, two who stand with it and three who read the matter differently, and answers each in his own words.

ABOUT THIS VOLUME

A Commentary That Argues a Position

This volume does not survey the readings of the Davidic covenant and leave the choice to the reader. It states what the author believes the passages teach, sets out the evidence from Samuel through the prophets, the Gospels and Acts to Hebrews and the Revelation, and answers the strongest objections in the objectors’ own terms, naming the modern writers who hold them.

It is the fifth volume of the Covenantal Commentary Series. The first four asked what God promised Abraham, who the seed is, what the line was given to possess, and what the whole is for; this one asks what God swore to David, and where the throne is. It is a different kind of book from the Digital Studies, which walk through a passage and report what the text says. Scripture is quoted from the King James Version throughout, and the writers named in Chapter 9 are quoted only from their own published words.

WHAT THE VOLUME COVERS

From Samuel to Revelation

The Promise and the Oath

Read 2 Samuel 7 beside 1 Chronicles 17, and Psalms 89 and 132, and ask what was promised, by what, and under what condition.

The Interrupted Throne

Follow Jeremiah, Ezekiel, Hosea, Amos and Haggai on the line put off the throne, and see what each says follows.

The Prophets’ Son of David

Read Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel and Zechariah on the child, the Branch and David my servant, and mark where each sets the throne.

The Son Announced

Trace the covenant’s words in Luke 1, the Gospels’ use of the title, and Paul’s mention of David’s seed.

The Throne in Acts

Read Acts 1, 2, 13 and 15 as written, and keep apart what each passage says and what it leaves unsaid.

Five Writers, Five Objections

Hear Woods and Johnson beside Wright, Kline and Horton, and see each objection answered.
