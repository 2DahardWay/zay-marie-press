The Remnant

The Preserved Israel of Prophecy, from Elijah’s Seven Thousand to the Kingdom

Is the remnant of the prophets Israel’s believing portion, or the church?

Through every judgment on the nation, God has kept a believing portion of Israel alive. The prophets name it the remnant of Jacob, the escaped, the left in Zion and the third part brought through the fire. Revelation shows it protected in the wilderness, hunted by the dragon and sealed with the Lamb on Zion. This commentary argues that the remnant is Israel’s from first to last, and defends the position from the whole Bible.

It follows the pattern from Noah, Joseph and the seven thousand through Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, Micah, Zephaniah, Zechariah, Joel and Daniel, reads the return from Babylon and the Lord’s ministry to the lost sheep of the house of Israel, and then Romans 9 to 11 and Galatians 6:16. It reads the tribulation remnant, the 144,000 and the male child of Revelation 12, Israel’s repentance and the kingdom, and gives the reasons the Body of Christ is not the remnant.

ABOUT THIS VOLUME

A Commentary That Argues a Position

This volume does not survey the views and leave the choice to the reader. It states what the author believes the remnant passages teach, sets out the evidence from Genesis to Revelation, and answers those who disagree where they are strongest, including the reading that makes the church the true Israel and the reading that places Jewish believers of this age inside the church.

It is the second volume of the Prophetic Identity Commentary Series, which asks who the people of the prophets and of Revelation are. It is a different kind of book from the Digital Studies, which walk through a passage and report what the text says. The volume gives the church its own full place, and asks only that the name of the remnant be left where the prophets put it. Scripture is quoted from the King James Version throughout.
