The Second Coming Map

The Wars, the Judgments and the Kingdom, in Order

When the King returns, Scripture describes wars He fights, judgments He holds and a kingdom He opens. This map puts them in order, shows who stands at each one and who is being judged, and marks where the order is Scripture’s own and where it is a reading. Choose any station below to open it.

This guide argues a position, and says so. The map begins at the end of the Tribulation. The Body of Christ is shown only at its own judgment seat, before; it is not at the events of the Coming, which belong to Israel and the nations. Each passage carries a tag:

Stated the verse says itShown by the context the surrounding passage shows itInferred the map’s own reading, joining passages

Who Is Where

The Judgments at a Glance

Five separate courts, each with its own seat, people and ground.

What the Map Does Not Claim

Where Scripture gives no order, no length or no identification, the map says so instead of filling it in.
