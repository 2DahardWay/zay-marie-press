DIGITAL STUDIES

Focused, Single-Question Biblical Studies

Each Digital Study examines one passage, doctrine, distinction, or interpretive question with publisher-grade clarity. Designed for readers who want deep, structured understanding without system drift, each study isolates one issue and traces it through Scripture within Acts Overlap Theology — Prophecy and Mystery kept distinct, Paul’s stewardship preserved, Israel’s program honored.

COMMENTARY COLLECTION

Whole-Bible Doctrinal Arguments

Where the Digital Studies teach how Scripture is built, each Commentary argues one doctrinal question from Genesis to Revelation. Each volume names the opposing views in their own terms, cites its sources, answers them with Scripture, and reaches a clear conclusion. Thirty volumes in six series.

BROWSE THE COMMENTARY COLLECTION
PARABLE STUDIES

Every Parable in Its Original Setting

Each Parable Study restores the parable to its gospel context — its audience, its occasion, and its Kingdom horizon. These studies read the parables inside Israel’s Prophecy Program rather than importing them into the Body of Christ. The result is clarity: each parable interpreted as Jesus delivered it, within the prophetic structure it originally addressed.

BROWSE PARABLE STUDIES
DOCTRINAL PACKETS

A Doctrinal Map for Every Book of Scripture

Each packet traces the book’s doctrine, its program placement (Prophecy or Mystery), its contribution to the biblical storyline, and includes charts and a teaching outline for fast, accurate study. Ideal for teachers, pastors, and serious students who want a concise, structured doctrinal overview of each biblical book without losing program identity.
