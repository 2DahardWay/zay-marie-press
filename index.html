OUR PUBLISHING PROGRAMS

ACTS OVERLAP THEOLOGY SERIES

Two Distinct Streams. One Overlap.

The Acts Overlap Theology Series examines the Book of Acts through the distinction between God's Prophecy Program for Israel and His Mystery Program for the Body of Christ, recognizing Acts 9–28 as the period in which the two programs operate concurrently—distinct, side-by-side, and divinely coordinated.
