# Digital Studies — Catalog Category Standard

Version 1.1 — publisher directive, 2026-10-01. Governs how every Digital Study is classified into one of the five primary catalog categories, and the cover label each category uses. Applies to all current studies and every new study. Each study has exactly one primary category, assigned by its controlling subject. Collections and bundles are separate and unaffected.

## Binding force (settled; do not re-ask)
This Standard is final and in force. The 71 assignments below are fixed rulings, not proposals. Claude does not ask the publisher where a study belongs, and does not reopen an assigned study, unless the publisher himself changes it. For a new study, Claude classifies it by the procedure below, places the card and cover label accordingly, and states the category and the one-line reason in its delivery message so the publisher can overrule. Changes to a ruling come only from the publisher, and are logged here with a version bump.

## Decision procedure for a new study
Apply the tests in order. The first test that fits decides the category.
1. **Method test → Foundations.** Does the study teach how Scripture is built or read: dispensation, stewardship, administration, right division, the Acts Overlap, covenant method (as opposed to covenant content), hermeneutical rules for Acts or for Israel's Scriptures, the two apostolic commissions, the categories of "church" or "Israel" as tools, or a comparison of interpretive systems? If yes → Foundations.
2. **Cross-program doctrine test → Doctrinal Themes.** Is the controlling subject one doctrinal concept or term traced across several passages and across both Prophecy and Mystery (adoption, election, circumcision, redemption, justification, baptism, judgments, crowns, resurrections, the book of life, the Bride, the filling of the Spirit, the names of "church", sanctification, security, the believer's walk, the eternal purpose, the eternal state)? If yes → Doctrinal Themes.
3. **Israel's program test → Israel & Prophecy.** Is the controlling subject Israel's national identity, covenant content, Kingdom gospel, prophetic chronology, the Tribulation and Revelation's prophetic program, the feasts, the remnant, Israel's blindness and restoration, Messiah's legal and prophetic credentials, or Romans 9–11 as Prophecy content (including the olive tree)? If yes → Israel & Prophecy, even when the study is titled by a Revelation or Gospel passage (precedents: 26, 61, 64, 71).
4. **Mystery and Body test → Body of Christ.** Is the controlling subject the Mystery revealed through Paul, the Body's beginning, identity, standing, calling, heavenly position, hope or conduct (grace instruction, Pauline commands, the Law's jurisdiction over the Body, the Day of Christ, the Body and the Tribulation, giving, prayer, the household), or one Pauline doctrine that is stated only of the Body (new creation, the Gospel of the Grace of God)? If yes → Body of Christ.
5. **Passage test → Passages.** Does the study's outline walk one passage or a tightly connected passage cluster verse by verse (a warning passage, a single saying, a single event, a single sermon, one prophecy sign), and does it fail tests 1–4? If yes → Passages.
If two tests fit, the earlier test wins, except that a study titled by a bare passage reference (a chapter and verse, or a named single passage) that does not fall squarely under test 1 goes to Passages unless the passage is Revelation's prophetic program or Messiah's credentials (test 3). If none fits, the study is Passages.

## Steps whenever a study is added or its category changes
1. Set the category by the procedure and state it (with one-line reason) in the delivery message.
2. Put the catalog card in that category's section of `digital-studies.html`, in number order. The pill counts update themselves from the cards.
3. Letter the cover label with the category's exact name (see Cover labels) in the cover's own style.
4. Add the study to the matching row of the Relationship Map's category table, bump the Map version and log it.
5. Add the study to the assignments table in this Standard, bump this Standard's version (rename the file to match) and log it here.
6. Collections, bundles and prices are separate and are not changed by a category.
Note: the small eyebrow on a card ("Digital Study" or "Biblical Doctrine") is cosmetic and is not tied to the category.

## The five categories

### 1. Foundations (catalog heading: Foundational Distinctions; pill: Foundations)
**Rule:** Studies that define how Scripture works — interpretive tools, structural concepts, hermeneutical controls, and dispensational architecture.
**Belongs:** What Is a Dispensation?; how to read Prophecy vs. Mystery; the Acts Overlap; right-division principles; studies explaining stewardship, revelation, administration, covenants (as method) or interpretive method; any study that teaches the framework rather than a specific passage or doctrinal theme.
**Does not belong:** passage-specific exegesis; Israel-specific prophetic content; Body-specific Pauline instruction; thematic doctrinal studies (e.g., adoption, election).

### 2. Israel & Prophecy (catalog heading: Israel, Prophecy & Covenants; pill: Israel & Prophecy)
**Rule:** Studies that explain Israel's prophetic identity, covenants, promises, national calling, prophetic chronology, or Old Testament-rooted program.
**Belongs:** Abrahamic Covenant; Davidic Covenant; New Covenant (Israel's restoration); remnant studies; prophetic timelines; Kingdom program; Israel's stumbling, diminishing, blindness and restoration; Romans 9–11 (Prophecy content); any study where Israel's national identity is the controlling subject.
**Does not belong:** Body of Christ identity; Mystery revelation; Pauline instruction for the Body; studies where Israel is mentioned only incidentally.

### 3. Body of Christ (catalog heading: The Body of Christ & Grace; pill: Body of Christ)
**Rule:** Studies that explain Pauline Mystery truth — the Body's identity, standing, calling, blessings, instruction, and heavenly position.
**Belongs:** The Law's Jurisdiction and the Body of Christ (Study 59); One Body / One New Man; heavenly places; grace instruction; Pauline commands; what is true of believers in Christ under the Mystery program.
**Does not belong:** Israel's covenants; prophetic promises; olive-tree participation; any study where the Body is only mentioned in passing.

### 4. Passages (catalog heading: Passages & Apostolic Context; pill: Passages)
**Rule:** Studies built around one specific passage or a tightly connected passage cluster, where the study's structure follows the text itself.
**Belongs:** a strictly passage-driven Romans 11 study; Galatians 2; Acts 9; Matthew 24; any study whose outline is "walk through the passage verse by verse".
**Does not belong:** thematic studies; covenant studies; identity studies; studies where the passage is used but not structurally controlling.

### 5. Doctrinal Themes (catalog heading: Doctrinal Terms & Themes; pill: Doctrinal Themes)
**Rule:** Studies that explain a single doctrinal concept across multiple passages — not tied to one passage and not limited to one program.
**Belongs:** Adoption (Study 52); Election (Study 50); Circumcision (Study 51); faith, grace, justification, sanctification when treated thematically; any study tracing a doctrine across Prophecy and Mystery without being passage-anchored.
**Does not belong:** passage-driven studies; program-identity studies; covenant-specific studies.

## Cover labels
The upper-right label on every cover is the exact name of the study's catalog category. Nothing shorter, nothing older.
- Single-line covers use the pill names: Foundations = FOUNDATIONS; Israel & Prophecy = ISRAEL & PROPHECY; Body of Christ = BODY OF CHRIST; Passages = PASSAGES; Doctrinal Themes = DOCTRINAL THEMES.
- Two-line covers (the Studies 60–71 series style) use the catalog heading names: FOUNDATIONAL / DISTINCTIONS; ISRAEL, PROPHECY / & COVENANTS; THE BODY OF CHRIST / & GRACE; PASSAGES & / APOSTOLIC CONTEXT; DOCTRINAL TERMS / & THEMES.
- New covers use the single-line pill name unless the publisher approves the two-line form.
- Retired, never used: RIGHT DIVISION, PROPHECY (alone), DOCTRINE, INTERPRETATION, BIBLICAL DOCTRINE, BIBLICAL INTERPRETATION.
When a study changes category, re-letter its cover in the cover's own style (same position, size, colour and tracking) and update the website jpg, webp, png and size variants. Study 53's label is two lines (DOCTRINAL / THEMES) because its light beam covers the left of a single line.
