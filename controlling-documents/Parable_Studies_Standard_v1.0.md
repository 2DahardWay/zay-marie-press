Date: 2026-10-16. Rule recorded as Master Standard v1.164 Rule 35. Source: the publisher's two documents, *THE OFFICIAL PARABLE STUDIES FORMAT (UPDATED)* and *THE OFFICIAL RULES For the Parable Studies* (Authoritative Hermeneutic Constitution), uploaded 2026-10-16. ## 1. Scope and mode

- Applies to every Parable Study (35 on the site). It replaces the earlier ten-page Parable Study layout when a study is rebuilt.
- Mode 1 (text-governed) with the Parable Rules below. The Framework rules that bind the Digital Studies bind these too (Rules 7, 11, 13–19, 32 and the wording rules); the "report as written" rules for specific passages (Rules 20–28, 30, 33) apply where a parable touches them.
- Plain-voice rule (Rule 34) governs the Conclusion, Key Distinctions and Final Synthesis: the parable and the text are the subject, not "the study".
- Reader-facing wording rule: no Framework, PAM, Master Standard, rule number, "firewall" or similar term reaches the reader, including inside diagrams and covers.

## 2. The 13 sections (in this order; none merged, none skipped — Rule 17, Rule 18)

1. **What You Will Learn** — a concise preview of the study's objectives: what the reader will trace in the narrative; what identifications Jesus gives (if any); what interpretive boundaries must be preserved; what the reader will not do (for example, import symbols). Bulleted (Master Standard v1.50), process and markers, never the answer (v1.24 preview standard).
2. **The Question and the Setting** — the immediate scene; the trigger question or remark; the social or prophetic context; why Jesus tells this parable here. Prevents the parable from floating free or being universalized.
3. **The Story in Its Sequence** (may be subdivided) — the characters, actions, turning points, contrasts and narrative logic, exactly as Jesus tells it; no added meaning.
4. **Jesus' Explanation (If Present)** — records only what Jesus explicitly identifies: named referents, a table of identifications, and a warning against adding referents He does not name. **Claude's setting (publisher chose "keep it", 2026-10-16):** where Jesus gives no explanation the section still stands, so that no section is skipped (Rule 18); it states that Jesus gives none and lists what He leaves unidentified (Rule 3).
5. **Prophetic Meaning of the Comparison (Without Identity Assignment)** — what the comparison communicates about the Kingdom; what the hearers in Jesus' prophetic audience are meant to understand; the force of the warning, reversal or valuation; the prophetic implications without assigning identities. No identity assignment, no symbol importation, no doctrinal transfer; only the meaning that arises from the comparison itself.
6. **Boundaries With Neighboring Studies** — differences in setting, characters, narrative movement and interpretive detail; prevents cross-parable contamination.
7. **Original Address and Responsible Present Learning** — Jesus' prophetic setting (pre-Acts); the Kingdom audience; the Acts Overlap boundary; the Mystery beginning with Paul (Acts 9); a principled lesson for the Body without identity transfer. Carries the three standing sentences (Rules 10, 11, 16).
8. **Conclusion and Teaching Summary** — the narrative sequence, the prophetic meaning, the interpretive boundaries and the present lesson, in plain voice.
9. **Key Distinctions to Retain** — what differs between characters, actions and settings; what must not be imported or assigned.
10. **Scripture-Tracing Exercise** — a structured tracing assignment; comparison with neighboring passages; a marking of what is stated and what is not.
11. **Review and Discussion** — questions tied to the narrative sequence, the interpretive boundaries, the prophetic meaning and the present learning.
12. **Teaching Outline** — the sequence of the parable, the prophetic meaning, the boundaries and the present lesson, ready for an instructor.
13. **Guided Further Study and Final Synthesis** — related passages; neighboring parables; a final synthesis paragraph; a reminder of the boundaries.

## 3. The Parable Rules (the publisher's 22, as given)

**Section 1 — Textual discipline.**
- Rule 1. Follow the narrative sequence exactly: the order Jesus gives, with no rearranging, merging of scenes or importing details from other passages.
- Rule 2. State only what the text states. May describe actions, motives (as the text gives them), contrasts, turning points, narrative logic. May not invent motives, backstories, symbolic referents or doctrinal meanings Jesus does not supply.
- Rule 3. Mark what Jesus does NOT identify. If He does not name a referent, say explicitly: "Jesus does not identify this object/person/group."

**Section 2 — Explanation.**
- Rule 4. Record Jesus' explicit identifications verbatim: list each, state it exactly, add no further referent.
- Rule 5. Create no identification Jesus does not give. No identity assignment unless Jesus names it. Absolute.

**Section 3 — Prophetic meaning.**
- Rule 6. May state the prophetic meaning of the comparison: the Kingdom meaning, the warning, the reversal, the valuation, the force of the comparison.
- Rule 7. May NOT assign identities to characters, objects or groups (for example: invited guests = Israel's leaders; poor/crippled/blind/lame = Gentiles; roads/hedges = Gentile mission; treasure = Israel; pearl = the Body; merchant = Christ; host = God the Father; servant = apostles) unless Jesus explicitly names them.
- Rule 8. May NOT import symbols from other parables (the field of Matthew 13:38 into 13:44; Matthew's garment inspection into Luke 14; Matthew's violent rejecters into Luke's excuses).
- Rule 9. State prophetic meaning ONLY at the level of the comparison. May say: "The Kingdom summons is ready." "Refusal has consequences." "The invitation expands outward." "The Kingdom is of surpassing worth." "Presumption does not guarantee participation." May NOT say: "This group represents X." "This object symbolizes Y." "This movement corresponds to Z."

**Section 4 — Program boundary.**
- Rule 10. Preserve the prophetic address; state: "Jesus speaks during His earthly Kingdom ministry before the Acts overlap."
- Rule 11. Preserve the Mystery boundary; state: "The Mystery of the Body of Christ begins with Paul's Acts 9 calling."
- Rule 12. Do NOT transfer Kingdom identity to the Body: the banquet, the treasure, the pearl, the field, the net, the sower, the mustard seed and the leaven are not the Body's identity or salvation.
- Rule 13. Do NOT collapse programs: Israel's prophetic program, the Body's Mystery program, the Acts overlap and the post-Acts suspension are not merged.

**Section 5 — Present learning.**
- Rule 14. May give a principled lesson for the Body: ethical principles, warnings, valuation, humility and stewardship lessons.
- Rule 15. May NOT transfer prophetic promises to the Body: the Kingdom feast is not the Body's destiny; prophetic reversals are not Mystery doctrine; Israel's warnings are not Body identity.
- Rule 16. State the boundary explicitly: "This is a principled lesson, not a transfer of Kingdom identity to the Body."

**Section 6 — Structure.**
- Rule 17. Follow the 13-section structure above.
- Rule 18. Keep each section distinct: no merging, no collapsing, no skipping.
- Rule 19. Publisher-grade clarity: clean prose, structured paragraphs, precise distinctions, doctrinally safe boundaries.

**Section 7 — Prohibitions.**
- Rule 20. Do NOT allegorize: no hidden meanings, symbolic systems or devotional conventions.
- Rule 21. Do NOT universalize: the treasure, the pearl, the banquet and the net are not universal salvation metaphors.
- Rule 22. Do NOT turn narrative actions into soteriology: "sell all," "come," "find," "seek," "buy," "enter," "refuse" are not mechanisms of salvation.

## 4. Claude's settings (for ruling; they apply to the pilots and change only on the publisher's word)

1. **Required sentences.** Rules 10, 11 and 16 give sentences to be stated; each appears in Section 7 of every study in these exact words, in a boxed callout so the reader finds them. Rule 3's sentence is used in the form "Jesus does not identify [the object/person/group]." wherever a referent is left unnamed, and the unnamed items are listed together in Section 4.
2. **Program statement for Mark and Luke parables.** Rule 10's sentence is used as given for every parable; where a parable is told in the Upper Room or on the final road the sentence is unchanged ("before the Acts overlap"), and the Framework's placement of Acts 1–8 stays outside these studies.
3. **Page geometry and type.** The Digital Studies geometry and Roboto typeface (Master Standard §6, §8): page 483.846 × 725.754 pt; body 10 pt; headings 17 pt navy; tables Slate Blue header; centered footer. Footer line: "ZAY-MARIE PRESS PARABLE STUDIES SERIES • N" (the footer wording is pending the publisher's confirmation). Page count follows content (publisher ruling, 2026-10-16); the earlier "10 pages including cover" line is replaced by the real count in each study.
4. **Callouts.** Up to five boxed callouts per study (Study Goal; the trigger question; the explanation or the "Jesus gives none" note; the three standing sentences; the final reminder of boundaries).
5. **Scripture.** KJV only; every quotation is checked against the KJV corpus (`build-tools/parables/kjvcheck.py`).
6. **Terms.** "Kingdom" and "the Kingdom of heaven" follow the passage's own wording; the "mysteries of the kingdom" (Matthew 13:11) are the Lord's Kingdom matters given to His disciples in His earthly ministry and are not the Mystery revealed through Paul (read with the Prophecy & Mystery volumes; no working term shown).
7. **Where a rule and the passage meet.** Where Jesus names a referent (Matthew 13:37–39), the study records it verbatim (Rule 4) and the identification belongs to Section 4 only; Section 5 does not extend it.

## 5. Checks run on every study

- 13 sections present, in order, each distinct; Section 4 present.
- Search for forbidden identity language (group = X) outside Jesus' own identifications; none found.
- Rule 3 sentence appears for every unnamed referent; Rules 10, 11, 16 sentences verbatim.
- No symbol imported across parables; neighbours are named only in Section 6 and Section 13.
- Wording rule grep (Framework, PAM, Program Assignment, Master Standard, firewall, "Rule ", anchor, packet) plus OCR of rendered pages.
- KJV check; Roboto-only fonts; page count recounted and recorded (site.js equivalent, page, previews) when the study is published.

## 6. File naming (publisher ruling, 2026-10-16)

Delivered files sort in study order in a folder: `Parable_Study_NN_Title_Words.pdf` and `.docx` (NN = the two-digit number of the site page, `parable-NN-slug.html`; the Sower and the Soils, whose page is `parable-sower.html`, takes 35). Pilot or review copies add `_REVIEW` before the extension; the approved file carries no suffix. Site preview images keep the site pattern `parable-NN-slug-page-P.jpg` (P = interior page number, PDF page minus 1): three per study, pages 1 (Table of Contents), 2 (What You Will Learn) and one page of Section 3 (Story in Its Sequence), 1041 x 1560 px. Same convention for all 35 studies.
