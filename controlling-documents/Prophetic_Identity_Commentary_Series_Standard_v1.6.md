# Prophetic Identity Commentary Series Standard

Version 1.6 — publisher directive, 2026-10-07. Governs the Prophetic Identity Commentary Series (each title a "Commentary Volume"), written in Mode 2 (canonical identity synthesis) under Framework §XX and Master Standard §20 Rule 29. It is modelled on the Prophetic Commentaries Series Standard (v1.7) and shares its layout, quotation and citation rules. The Digital Studies stay under Mode 1 and the Master Standard; nothing here changes them. This Standard records the series and introduces no new rule, so no rule-sync is needed for it; the modern-sources-only rule it follows is already in CLAUDE.md and Master Standard v1.69.

## Mode and rules
- Mode 2. Rules 7, 11 and 13–19 bind; Rules 8 and 20–28 do not apply to a commentary volume. The mode label never appears in a volume, the website copy or the previews. A volume opens with a plain statement that it argues a position and defends it from the whole canon.
- Doctrinal controls: the Body is never Israel or the remnant; Romans 11 receives the full Olive Tree treatment (participation is not identity, blessing is not covenant, standing is not program membership); no shared ground of salvation; the Body participates in no Israelite covenant; the Body is absent from Daniel's seventieth week, Revelation and the Day of the Lord.
- Reader-facing wording: the words Framework, PAM, Program Assignment, Master Standard, rule numbers, firewall, anchor and the mode labels never appear in the volume, the website copy or any preview. The non-transfer rule is called "the principle of no transfer" and is defined in the text. Check the text and run an OCR pass over every rendered page and preview image.

## Settled rulings for Volume 1 (publisher, 2026-10-06)
- "Bride" and "the Lamb's wife" are Israel's: the wife is redeemed Israel, and the New Jerusalem carries her name, as in Isaiah 54 and 62.
- Ephesians 5:22–33 and 2 Corinthians 11:2 are read as a marriage pattern taught to the Body, not as a Bride title.
- The guests of Revelation 19:9 are the friends of the bridegroom: the faithful of Israel's prophetic hope, distinct from the wife, never the Body.

## Quotation and citation (binding)
- Scripture is quoted from the KJV only, and every quotation is checked against the KJV text (two independent copies compared, merged into a corpus file, and a script tests every quoted string and table row).
- Cite by passage, not by page. A modern writer is quoted only from words actually read in a free full text (text the publisher supplies, the author's own free articles or sermon transcripts, or a free full commentary); otherwise the position is described in Claude's own words naming author and work. The publisher is never asked to buy, borrow or consult a book. Pirated copies are never used. Up to five modern voices, no minimum.
- Modern sources only (Master Standard v1.69, Rule 29): old commentaries and authors from before 1900 are not cited, quoted or listed. Where a modern work is used, quotations are short and exact, and every quoted sentence is confirmed on two fetches.
- Opponents are quoted accurately and answered from the text; no view is attributed to a writer unless words read state it.
- The bibliography gives author, title, publisher and year, with a one-line flag only where a view is described and not quoted.

## Structure
1. Cover (page 1), then a Table of Contents. 2. Preface. 3. Thesis and the passages it rests on. 4. Argument chapters, each opening with its claim and closing with a callout. 5. Objections and replies. 6. Clause-by-clause tables. 7. Conclusion with a one-table restatement. 8. Appendices. 9. Bibliography, then the end imprint on the same page.
- Page and type geometry follow Master Standard §6 (page 9677 × 14515 twips, Roboto 10 pt, margins top 864, bottom 1224, sides 1031; H1 Roboto Bold 17 pt navy #1B3350, 12 pt before and 8 pt after; tables and callouts 7615 twips wide with a 100-twip indent; table header fill #3B6E91; callout fill #E3EDF7 with a 1 pt navy border; 10 pt spacing around tables; no blank spacer paragraphs; the cover is a full-bleed section with no footer).
