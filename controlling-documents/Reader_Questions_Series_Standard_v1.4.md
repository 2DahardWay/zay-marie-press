Governs the Reader Questions Series: short, general questions Christian readers ask about one verse or passage, answered in **Mode 3 (contextual exegesis)** under Master Standard §20 Rule 31. Modes: Digital Studies = Mode 1; Commentary Series = Mode 2; Reader Questions Series = Mode 3. The mode label is a working term and never appears in anything the reader sees (Rule 14).

## Ruled by the publisher (2026-10-10)
- **Purpose:** to give the reader a sound exegetical interpretation of what a verse says in the context of its pericope and the preceding chapter(s), not to leave the question open. Whether the reader agrees is the reader's matter.
- **Layers:** free short answers with paid full volumes. The free page carries a short answer (two or three sentences), one context paragraph, the passage, related links and a line pointing to the paid volume. The paid volume carries the full entry (answer, context, labelled evidence, other readings, what it means, related reading), two to three interior pages (ruled 2026-10-10: enough to make the case), with a bibliography of modern writers.
- **Price (ruled 2026-10-10): $7.99 a volume**, between a Digital Study ($5.99) and a Commentary Volume ($14.99). Volumes stay outside the Digital Studies catalog, bundles and Library Edition. **Placement (ruled 2026-10-10):** a separate Questions page. Any bundle: to be ruled. **Volume size (ruled 2026-10-10):** about ten to twelve entries a volume, which at two to three pages an entry is about thirty pages, between a Digital Study and a Commentary Volume.
- **Binding rules:** Rules 7, 11 and 13–19 bind; Rules 8, 12, 20–28 and 30 do not. Rules 9 and 10 apply.
- **Positions (amended 2026-10-10):** Claude drafts every entry by the Framework, recorded rulings and the passage's own context without asking for a ruling first; each entry notes which positions are settled and which are Claude's reading; each volume draft carries one short "needs your eye" list (Framework silent, or two readings both fit); the publisher corrects only what he disagrees with and each ruling is recorded below.
- **Sources:** passage citation and modern sources only (Rule 29) for paid entries; KJV text verified word for word from a source before it is published.
- **Cross-links:** a free page or entry that links a Digital Study says plainly that the study reports the text as written and the entry argues a position.

## Volume format (approved 2026-10-10 from the sample page)
Roboto only; body 10 pt black (6 pt after, 1.08); entry titles and major headings 17 pt navy bold; sub-headings 12 pt navy bold; the answer in a boxed callout; KJV tables (Verse / KJV text / What to observe) and a readings-compared table with Slate Blue headers and white body rows, full text width, 10 pt before and after; study page size (483.846 × 725.754 pt) and margins; centered footer "ZAY-MARIE PRESS READER QUESTIONS SERIES, VOLUME V • N"; covers 1600 × 2400 with "VOLUME V" upper right and "READER QUESTIONS SERIES" at the bottom. The series name and cover lines are working assumptions until the publisher confirms them. KJV text is checked with `build-tools/commentary/kjvcheck.py` and the browser check.

## Entry form (full entry)
1. The answer (one or two plain sentences).
2. The context: the pericope and the preceding chapter(s).
3. What carries the weight: each point marked **Stated**, **Shown by the context** or **Inferred**.
4. The other readings, stated fairly, with named modern writers; why this answer is preferred.
5. What it means for the reader.
6. Related reading (Digital Studies, Commentary Series, Parable Studies, Doctrinal Packets; up to three each).

## Points the paid entry on 2 Thessalonians 2:3 must answer
- Acts 21:21 (the same noun of forsaking Moses) and 1 Timothy 4:1 (the related verb); the article in "the falling away" and "first".
- The restrainer's grammar: neuter in 2:6, masculine in 2:7; the rival reading that the restrainer is the Holy Spirit.
- Named modern writers on both sides (apostasy readings are the larger group among modern commentaries; departure readings a smaller one), each checked under the sources rule before any quotation.
