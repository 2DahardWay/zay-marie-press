### Study 49 — The Resurrections of Scripture — CONFLICT (website wording)

- **Where:** `study-49-the-resurrections-of-scripture.html`, "About This Study", third paragraph.
- **Study says:** "nor of the rapture passages in Matthew 24 and Luke 17, which Study 29 traces in their own setting."
- **PAM says:** Luke 17 (Days of the Son of Man) = "Kingdom judgment", "Prophecy Program". Editor Rule 4: "Never merge programs."
- **Why it conflicts:** Calling the Luke 17 "taken" scene a "rapture passage" reads it as the Body's gathering. That contradicts the PAM assignment. It also contradicts Study 29, whose About paragraph says being "taken" in this scene means judgment overtaking the unprepared, "not the Body's gathering to Christ", and Study 29's catalog card, which says this "is not the Rapture of the Body of Christ." The Study 49 related-studies entry for Study 29 already uses the correct framing ("the prophetic separation traced there").
- **Scope on the website:** the phrase appears in this one place only. It is not in `study-connections.html` or `digital-studies.html`.
- **Not yet checked:** whether the interior PDF of Study 49 carries the same sentence.
- **Fix (approved by publisher to make; applied locally, NOT pushed):** replace "the rapture passages in Matthew 24 and Luke 17" with "the 'one taken, one left' passages in Matthew 24 and Luke 17".

## Publisher ruling on Study 35 (2026-09-30)

Galatians 6:16 refers to believing Jews within the Body of Christ, not a separate remnant outside the Body. The PAM wording stands. Believing Jews remain Israel by identity but are not a distinct programmatic group during the present administration. "The remnant is never the Body" refers to identity, not program membership: "remnant" is an ethnic-covenantal category, not an administrative one; believing Jews are gathered into the Body during the Mystery program. Study 35 is to be revised so it does not imply believing Israel exists outside the Body in the present age. It may keep the ethnic distinction (Israel remains Israel) but must affirm these believing Jews belong to the Body and are not a separate prophetic remnant during the current administration.
