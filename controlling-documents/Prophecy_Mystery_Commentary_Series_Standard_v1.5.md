# Prophecy & Mystery Commentary Series Standard

Version 1.5 — publisher directive, 2026-10-07. Governs the Prophecy & Mystery Commentary Series (each title a "Commentary Volume"), written in Mode 2 (canonical identity synthesis) under Framework §XX and Master Standard §20 Rule 29. It is modelled on the Covenantal Commentary Series Standard (v1.5), the Prophetic Commentary Series Standard (v1.10) and the Prophetic Identity Commentary Series Standard (v1.8) and shares their layout, quotation and citation rules. The Digital Studies stay under Mode 1 and the Master Standard; nothing here changes them. This Standard records the series and introduces no new rule of its own beyond the series record itself; it follows the modern-sources-only rule (v1.69), the commentary cover-size and numbering rules (v1.70), the verse-level Scripture index rule (v1.73) and the objections chapter standard (v1.76).

### Overlap with other work
Volume 1 shares ground with Digital Study 4 (Israel and the Body of Christ), Covenantal Commentary Volume 4 (one purpose, two callings) and the Prophetic Identity Commentary Series (bride, remnant, New Covenant, olive tree, Jerusalem above). The studies are Mode 1 and report the text; the volumes are Mode 2 and argue a position. A volume cross-refers to its neighbours and does not repeat their argument. Never apply a volume's conclusions inside a Digital Study.

## Volume 1 — Israel & the Body (thesis, outline and six rulings approved 2026-10-07)
**Title and subtitle.** Israel & the Body — Two Programs, Two Identities.

**Thesis.** Scripture runs two programs. Israel's is prophetic: national, covenantal, earthly, with the Kingdom for its hope. The Body's is the Mystery, hid in God and revealed through Paul: one new man in Christ, with its blessing, seat and hope in the heavenly places. The two ran concurrently through Acts 9–28 and never merged, transferred or absorbed one another. Israel's program was nationally suspended at Acts 28, not replaced. The Body is not Israel, and Israel is not the Body.

**Outline.** Preface; Thesis; ch. 1 how to tell whose passage you are reading (audience, program, concurrency); ch. 2 Israel's program (election, covenants, the gospel of the Kingdom, the Spirit's work, the earthly hope); ch. 3 the Body's program (the Mystery, the gospel of the grace of God, one body, the heavenly places); ch. 4 two origins (Abraham and Sinai; Acts 9 and Paul's revelation); ch. 5 covenants and gospels compared; ch. 6 calling, hope and destiny compared; ch. 7 the overlap (Acts 9–28, Acts 15, Acts 28); ch. 8 where the two are confused (Romans 11, Galatians 3 and 6:16, Ephesians 2); ch. 9 objections and replies; conclusion; appendices A–F (the last "Where the Passages Are Read"); bibliography.

**Rulings (publisher, 2026-10-07, "i approve"):**
1. Acts 9–28 is concurrency: "Concurrency is operational, not transformational."
2. Acts 28 is the national suspension of Israel's program, not a transfer, relocation or absorption.
3. Galatians 6:16, "the Israel of God": believing Jews within Israel's prophetic identity, not members of the Body.
4. Ephesians 2:11–22: the Body's one new man; the wall is abolished for the Body; no merger with Israel.
5. Galatians 2:7–9: two concurrent ministries, each content stated as written; no "two gospels by audience" wording (Rule 18 guard applies).
6. Romans 11 and Galatians 3 carry the standing sentences ("Participation is not identity. Blessing is not covenant. Standing is not program membership."; "The seed is prophetic fulfillment in Christ; the Body receives blessing through Christ alone, not through participation in the Abrahamic covenant.").

Footer and cover bottom line: "PROPHECY & MYSTERY COMMENTARY SERIES". The Commentary Collection tile wording "without overlap or replacement" is changed to "without merging or replacement".

**Objections chapter voices.** Supporting: J. C. O'Hair, David M. Havard, the Berean Bible Society's published transcript on the distinctions between prophecy and the mystery, and Jonathan Pratt (Galatians 6:16 only). Opposing: N. T. Wright, John Piper, Michael Horton, Meredith Kline. Cornelius Stam and Charles Baker were sought; no free English full text of either was found (Stam's free commentaries are in Spanish only; *Things That Differ* is sold), so neither is quoted.

## Volume 2 — The Mystery Program (thesis, outline and rulings approved 2026-10-07)
**Title and subtitle.** The Mystery Program — Hid in God, Revealed Through Paul.

**Thesis.** Scripture names a secret "kept secret since the world began" and "hid in God," made known to Paul by revelation. It is not Israel's prophetic program, and the prophets did not foretell it. Its content: Jews and Gentiles made fellowheirs and "of the same body" in Christ, "Christ in you, the hope of glory," a seat in the heavenly places. Paul was entrusted with it as a stewardship (oikonomia). It began with the revelation at Damascus (Acts 9), ran alongside Israel's program through Acts 9–28 and continues after Acts 28. The word "mystery" has other uses in Scripture; only this one is the Mystery.

**Outline.** Preface; Thesis; ch. 1 what "mystery" means, every use sorted; ch. 2 hid in God; ch. 3 revealed to Paul; ch. 4 the content of the Mystery; ch. 5 the stewardship and its boundaries; ch. 6 the Mystery and the prophets (Acts 3:21 beside Romans 16:25 and Ephesians 3:5); ch. 7 the Mystery through the overlap and beyond Acts 28; ch. 8 where it is confused (Matthew 13; Romans 11:25; Ephesians 5:32; Revelation 10:7 and 17:5; 2 Thessalonians 2:7; 1 Timothy 3:16; Pentecost as the start); ch. 9 objections and replies; conclusion; appendices A–F (the last "Where the Passages Are Read"); bibliography.

**Rulings (publisher, 2026-10-07, "i approve"):**
1. The Mystery Program begins at Acts 9; the revelation to Paul continues through his ministry.
2. The Mystery continues beyond Acts 28; Paul "the prisoner" is reported without dating the prison letters.
3. Romans 11:25's "mystery" is Prophecy Program content (Israel's blindness) under the standing Romans 11 sentences; Matthew 13, Revelation 10:7 and 17:5 belong to the Kingdom and Revelation settings, where the Body is absent.
4. 1 Corinthians 15:51 is the Body's own change and resurrection, revealed as a secret.
5. A (Romans 16:26 "by the scriptures of the prophets"): the Spirit-given prophetic writings by which the Mystery is now made known (Ephesians 3:5, "apostles and prophets by the Spirit"); the verse does not say the Old Testament prophets foretold it.
6. B (Ephesians 5:32 "a great mystery… concerning Christ and the church"): the Body's union with Christ as head and body, one flesh; not the bride identity, which the Framework gives to Israel.
7. The Body is not Israel and takes part in no Israelite covenant; Rule 18 governs Paul's ministry and Rule 19 the ground of salvation.

## Volume 3 — The Kingdom Program (thesis, outline and rulings approved 2026-10-07)
**Title and subtitle.** The Kingdom Program — Promised to Israel, Earthly, and Still to Come.

**Thesis.** Scripture promises Israel a kingdom: the Davidic King on David's throne at Jerusalem, the nations brought under Israel's restored line. It is earthly and is the subject of the prophets from Genesis to Malachi. John, the Lord and the Twelve announced it as "at hand" (Matthew 3:2; 4:17; 10:5–7). It was rejected by Israel's rulers and offered again in Acts 3:19–21; the offer ran through Acts 1–8, and Israel's program was nationally suspended at Acts 28. The kingdom is still to come: the Tribulation, the return of the King and the thousand years (Matthew 24:14; Revelation 11:15; 19:11–20:6). It is not the church and not the Body's heavenly calling; the Body is not Israel and has no part in Israel's covenants.

**Outline.** Preface; Thesis; ch. 1 what the kingdom is and how Scripture names it; ch. 2 the kingdom in the Law and the Prophets; ch. 3 the Davidic covenant and the throne; ch. 4 the kingdom announced and offered (Gospels); ch. 5 the kingdom offered again (Acts 1–8); ch. 6 the kingdom nationally suspended (Acts 28); ch. 7 the kingdom's return (Tribulation, the King's coming, the thousand years); ch. 8 the kingdom and the Body, where the two are confused; ch. 9 objections and replies (two to four supporting modern writers beside named modern opponents, closing table); conclusion; appendices (the Kingdom at a glance, the words, questions, terms, key passages in full, Where the Passages Are Read); bibliography.

**Rulings (publisher, 2026-10-07, "i approve", all four as recommended):**
1. The kingdom is offered through Acts 1–8 and Israel's program is nationally suspended at Acts 28; the offer is not withdrawn at Matthew 12.
2. Colossians 1:13 ("translated us into the kingdom of his dear Son") is deliverance into Christ's own rule, not entry into Israel's Davidic kingdom; the Body has no share in the Davidic kingdom.
3. Luke 17:21 is "in the midst of you" (the King present); Romans 14:17 describes Spirit-wrought conduct, not the program.
4. 1 Corinthians 6:9–10, Galatians 5:21 and Ephesians 5:5 ("shall not inherit the kingdom of God") are reported as written in their own settings and are not made warnings that remove Body membership.
5. (Standing, from Volumes 1–2) The Body is not Israel; Romans 11 under the standing sentences; Rule 18 governs Paul's ministry; Rule 19 the ground of salvation; the Galatians 3 standing sentence governs any treatment of the seed.

**Cover.** Publisher's art received 2026-10-07 (4096 × 6144, true 2:3); "Made with AI" badge and top-right corner to be repaired; the Hebrew lettering on the banners and the stone is decorative pseudo-Hebrew (publisher to decide: leave, blur or replace).

## Mode and rules
- Mode 2. Rules 7, 11 and 13–19 bind; Rules 9 and 10 apply where a settled ruling is stated and where the review is made; Rules 8, 12 and 20–28 do not bind. The mode label never appears in anything the reader sees.
- Every identification follows the Identity Synthesis Protocol; the Body is never Israel or the remnant; the Body participates in no Israelite covenant; Romans 11 receives the full Olive Tree treatment where it is read; the Galatians 3 standing sentence governs every treatment of the seed; Rule 18 governs Paul's synagogue ministry; Rule 19 governs the ground of salvation.
- Reader-facing wording: the words Framework, PAM, Program Assignment, Master Standard, rule numbers, firewall, anchor and the mode labels never appear in the volume, the website copy, any preview or any text inside an image. The non-transfer rule is called "the principle of no transfer". OCR every rendered page, the cover and every preview image.

## Quotation and citation (binding)
Scripture is KJV only, every quotation checked against the Gutenberg KJV. Cite by passage, not by page. A modern writer is quoted only from words actually read in a free full text; otherwise described in Claude's own words with no quotation marks. Modern sources only (20th century and later). Opponents are quoted accurately and answered from the text; where a supporter says more than the volume, the volume says so. The bibliography gives author, title, publisher and year, with a one-line flag where a view is described and not quoted or a source is a transcript or reprint.

## Volume 4 — The Resurrections of Scripture (thesis, outline and rulings approved 2026-10-08)
**Title and subtitle.** The Resurrections of Scripture — Christ’s, Israel’s and the Body’s Rising, and the Order Between Them. (Changed 2026-10-08 from “Two Programs, Two Resurrections”, which echoed the subtitle wording of Digital Study 49.)

**Thesis.** Scripture names more than one resurrection, and the passages that name them do not run together: Christ’s, the firstfruits, a rising to a life that does not end; Israel’s, the hope of the prophets and the Lord, with the just raised when the King comes and the rest of the dead after the thousand years; and the Body’s, a secret shown through Paul, in which the dead in Christ are raised and the living changed to meet the Lord in the air. The Body is not in Israel’s first resurrection and Israel is not in the Body’s.

**Outline.** Preface; Thesis; 1 What a Resurrection Is; 2 Christ the Firstfruits; 3 Israel’s Hope in the Law, the Psalms and the Prophets; 4 The Gospels and the Acts; 5 The Body’s Resurrection; 6 Revelation 20 and the First Resurrection; 7 The Order, in One Table; 8 Where the Resurrections Are Blurred; 9 Objections and Replies; Conclusion; Appendices A–D (at a glance, the words, study questions, terms), E (thirty-eight key passages in full), F (verse-level index, 152 rows); Bibliography.

**Rulings (publisher, 2026-10-08).** (1) The Body’s resurrection and change (1 Corinthians 15:51–52; 1 Thessalonians 4:16–17) precede Daniel’s seventieth week and the day of the Lord, with 1 Thessalonians 5:9 and the “they/you” grammar of 5:1–9 in support. (2) The first resurrection of Revelation 20:4–6 is Israel’s prophetic line, including those killed under the beast; the Body is not in it. (3) Daniel 12:2 and Isaiah 26:19 are Israel’s, not the Body’s. (4) Ezekiel 37 is a national restoration, not an individual resurrection. (5) The “one taken, one left” passages (Matthew 24; Luke 17) are Israel’s Tribulation separation, not the Body’s gathering. (6) 1 Corinthians 15:23 is read narrowly as the Body’s own order, within the mystery that verse 51 introduces. (7) Matthew 27:52–53 is reported as written, with no identification of the saints and no doctrine drawn from it. (8) Acts 24:15 and 26:6–8 are Paul speaking of the promise to the fathers, audience-specific under the standing rule on Paul’s synagogue ministry. (9) Philippians 3:11 is Paul’s own pursuit of the Body’s resurrection. (10) Hebrews 11:35, “a better resurrection,” is read within Israel’s line. (11) The Body’s resurrection is to heavenly glorification; Israel’s is to the earthly kingdom.

**Voices (chapter 9).** Two modern voices that stand with the reading in part (John F. Walvoord; the Berean Bible Society’s published transcript) and three that differ (Sam Storms; Dennis E. Johnson; Dean Davis), each quoted only from words read in a free full text, and the objectors’ fair points granted in section 9.6.
