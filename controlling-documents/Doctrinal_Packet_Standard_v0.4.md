# Zay-Marie Press Doctrinal Packet Standard — v0.4

Status: DRAFT. Only §7 (Preview Rule) has been explicitly approved by the publisher (2026-09-29). The remaining sections are the working draft used to build the Genesis pilot packet and still await the publisher's full review. Doctrinal Packets are a separate product strand from the Digital Studies; this Standard applies to packets only and never to Digital Studies (Master Standard §16 and §17 govern studies).

## 1. Purpose
A Doctrinal Packet is the paid, interpretive companion to a book's free Bible Book Summary. The free summary orients (setting, audience, structure, themes). The packet interprets: selective exegesis of the doctrinally decisive passages, theological architecture, Acts Overlap integration, and teaching-ready outlines.

## 2. Product specification
- One packet per book of the Bible. Price $2.99. Printable PDF.
- 3–5 pages; the final page count is fixed per packet at approval, as the Master Standard does for studies.
- No verse-by-verse exegesis. Selective exegesis of decisive passages; structural and thematic summary for the rest.
- Naming: "Doctrinal Packet — [Book]" with a one-line subtitle naming the book's doctrinal role.

## 3. Required sections, in this order
1. Header and metadata: book, testament, genre, theological category, Acts Overlap classification (Prophecy Program / Mystery Program / foundation), primary doctrinal axes.
2. Summary statement: one sentence.
3. Anchor texts (KJV): 4–8 verses maximum.
4. Selective exegesis: 3–5 decisive passages, 4–5 bullets each.
5. Theological architecture: foundations, program placement, what the book does and does not reveal.
6. Prophecy vs. Mystery / Israel vs. Body of Christ: what the book establishes and what it does not disclose.
7. Logic chain: numbered steps ending in a "Therefore" line.
8. Cross-reference network, grouped by theme, each reference labeled by program (Prophecy or Mystery).
9. Charts and timelines: at least one true chart or diagram (see §6).
10. Apologetic points.
11. Teaching outline, classroom-ready.
12. Application and closing summary.
(Sections 5, 6 and 10 may share a page where the book is short.)

## 3a. Governance
- Doctrine follows the Framework Control Document (Acts Overlap Theology) in full. Terms: Prophecy Program, Mystery Program, Acts Overlap, national judicial suspension at Acts 28.
- Before drafting: overlap check against the relationship map and the neighbouring studies. Each packet states in one sentence what it adds beyond the related Digital Studies.
- A program label is required for every cross-reference, so later Pauline texts are not presented as if the earlier book revealed them.
- Doctrinal wording needs publisher sign-off before anything is pushed, same as any doctrinal change.

## 4. Scripture quotation rules (KJV)
- Translation: KJV throughout, matching the Digital Studies series. The KJV is public domain, so no copyright notice and no quotation limits apply.
- Verse text is checked word for word against a KJV source before review; never quoted from memory.
- House rule: quote only what the argument needs. Anchor texts quoted in full; other passages cited by reference and paraphrased, so the packet stays within 3–5 pages.

## 5. Design
- Typography and colors follow the Digital Studies series, including Slate Blue (#3B6E91) table headers with high-contrast light text (Master Standard §13).
- Blue text is always the established dark navy.
- Cover (publisher directive, 2026-10-04): page 1 is a genre cover. A photograph chosen for the book's genre fills the top of the page and fades into a dark panel, deep blue for the Old Testament and deep green for the New Testament, carrying the gold "DOCTRINAL PACKET" label, the testament line, the title in white serif, a short gold rule, the italic subtitle, "ZAY-MARIE PRESS" and "Scripture: King James Version". The same genre photographs run on the packet pages and the hub. The cover is page 1, followed by the 4 content pages (5 pages in all). Replaces the earlier typographic title-block cover.

## 6. Charts
Charts are drawn as real tables or diagrams, not arrow strings in text. Every packet includes at least one covenant/promise table and one flow or timeline.

## 7. Preview Rule (approved by the publisher, 2026-09-29; applies to Doctrinal Packets only)
- A packet's website preview shows about one page's worth of the product, in proportion to a study preview (3 of about 16 pages, roughly 19%). It is never most of the packet.
- Show two partial views, both cropped from the final PDF: (1) the top of page 1, from the title block through the metadata table and the KJV anchor texts, stopping before the exegesis; (2) the chart section (covenant/promise chart and program flow) from the page where it falls, cropped to begin below any "Therefore" line.
- Never preview the exegesis, the theological architecture, the Prophecy-vs.-Mystery contrast, the logic chain, the teaching outline, or the closing summary.
- Preview text states what the reader will see and use, not the doctrinal conclusion (the spirit of Master Standard §17).
- Each preview is at most two cards. Images are named `packet-<book>-preview-N.jpg`, taken from the final PDF at 1041 px wide.
