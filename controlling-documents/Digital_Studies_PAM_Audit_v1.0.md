## Rules

  - Romans 11:25 = Prophecy Program content occurring during the Mystery administration.
  - 2 Corinthians 3:6 = the new covenant stays with Israel/Judah, and Paul's ministry stays with the Mystery.

  - Luke 21 states the AD 70 section and the future section, and reports the text neutrally where the division is debated.
  - Ephesians 2:14 uses the PAM wording "abolished for the Body" (governing program vs. wall removed).
  - Galatians 3: seed = prophetic fulfillment; Gentiles participate spiritually; remnant = Israel only.
  - Acts 10–11 (Cornelius) = a Prophecy Program event inside the Overlap; Acts 15 = Overlap, not assignable to a single program.
