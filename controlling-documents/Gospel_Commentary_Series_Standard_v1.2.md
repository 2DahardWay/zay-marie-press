## Ruled by the publisher (2026-10-09)
- **Name:** Gospel Commentary Series.
- **Scope:** five volumes on the four Gospels, each built around one narrower question, not a verse-by-verse walk through a Gospel, so the series reads the Lord's ministry before the Cross through the two-program lens (what was told and done for Israel). Where a volume enters ground already argued in another series or in a Digital Study, it cites that work as a neighbour and does not repeat the argument.
- **Length:** 50–70 pages including the cover. **Imprint:** Zay-Marie Press. **Translation:** KJV only. **Typeface:** Roboto.
- **Price:** $14.99 per volume unless the publisher says otherwise. Series bundle $54.99 (individually $74.95; save $19.96). The Complete Commentary Library price for 35 volumes is **not yet ruled**; until the publisher rules it and the series is added to the Commentary Collection page, the page keeps the 30-volume price ($274.99).
- **Footer:** "ZAY-MARIE PRESS GOSPEL COMMENTARY SERIES, VOLUME V • N" (page number restarts at 1 on the Table of Contents page).
- **Cover:** "VOLUME V" upper right, the title, and "GOSPEL COMMENTARY SERIES" at the bottom; 1600 × 2400 px artwork (2:3), 1024 × 1536 px website covers (.jpg and .webp), full-bleed interior cover page. Artwork supplied by the publisher; Claude removes any badge and sets any missing lettering.
- **Citation and sources:** passage citation, modern sources only, verse-level index "Where the Passages Are Read" as the last appendix before the Bibliography (Master Standard §20 Rule 29).
- **Placement:** Commentary Collection page (`commentary.html#gospel`), outside the Digital Studies catalog, bundles and Library Edition. No separate series page. Volume slugs: `commentary-gospel-0N-<slug>.html`.
- **Binding rules:** Rules 7, 11 and 13–19 bind; Rules 8, 12 and 20–28 do not.
- **Objections chapter:** the Covenantal pattern (two to four modern writers who fortify the thesis beside named modern writers who hold the opposite view, each objection in the opponent's own words, answered from Scripture, closing table).

## The volumes (titles and subtitles approved 2026-10-09)
1. **The Sermon on the Mount: The Kingdom's Standard for Israel** — subtitle: *The Kingdom's Standard for Israel, Not the Body of Christ* (cover title: The Sermon on the Mount). Matthew 5–7; Luke 6. Narrower question: to whom is each part addressed, and what does the text say it is for? (Study 62 covers only 5:17–20.)
2. **The Lord's Ministry to Israel: Sent Only to the Lost Sheep** — subtitle: *Why the Lord's Earthly Ministry Was to the Nation*. Matthew 10:5–6; 15:24; Romans 15:8. Narrower question: what does each Gospel report the Lord and the Twelve being sent to do, and to whom? The objections chapter takes the Samaritan woman, the centurion, the Greeks in John 12 and Luke 4:25–27. Avoid "not universal" wording (it can read as denying the Cross's reach).
3. **The Olivet Discourse: Israel's Tribulation, Israel's Signs, Israel's King** — subtitle: *The Kingdom Horizon of the King's Last Teaching to His Disciples* ("final public teaching" rejected: the discourse was private, Matthew 24:3; Mark 13:3). Matthew 24–25, Mark 13, Luke 21 side by side; Luke 21 under the standing neutral ruling. Narrower question: what does each account say, in order, and what is left open?
4. **What the Twelve Understood: The Road to the Cross** — subtitle: *How the Twelve Heard the Prophets, and What Was Not Yet Given* ("Missed the Mystery" rejected as anachronistic; the text says they missed the sufferings before the glory, Luke 18:34; 24:25–27; "…and Missed the Cross" is an acceptable alternative). Narrower question: what does each Gospel report the disciples hearing, grasping and missing?
5. **The Upper Room** — John 13–17; subtitle *The King's Last Words to the Twelve Before the Cross* ("His Nation" rejected: the setting is the eleven). Title and subtitle approved 2026-10-09. Narrower question: to whom are these promises given, and in what setting? Publisher rulings, 2026-10-09: (a) **John 14:2–3** is reported as written, to the eleven in the Upper Room, and placed in the Prophecy line's hope with no claim that it is the Body's gathering; the Body's gathering passages are named only as a contrast; (b) **John 15, the vine** is read within Israel's line (the vine of Psalm 80, Isaiah 5, Jeremiah 2:21), spoken to the eleven; the branch warnings stay with them and are not made warnings to the Body, and the Romans 11 olive-tree sentences are not drawn in; (c) **John 17:20–21** ("them also which shall believe on me through their word") is read as the believers who come through the Twelve's word (Acts 1–8 and the circumcision ministry); the Body is not named in it, and the passage is left open on anything beyond that. John 14:16–17 stays under the earlier Spirit ruling. Thesis and outline still to be approved before drafting.

Alternatives held in reserve: John's signs and "that ye might believe" (20:31); the Gospel accounts of the resurrection. Volumes are numbered 1–5 within the series. Each volume's thesis, outline and rulings are approved before drafting.

## Drafting cautions
- The Gospels are read as the Lord's ministry to Israel before the Cross; the Body and the Mystery are not read back into them (Rules 13–17).
- Study 75 and Rule 23 govern faith before the Cross; Rule 19 applies (no later revelation added to earlier believers).
- John 14:16–17 and other Spirit promises: the publisher's earlier rulings stand (Prophecy Program; "shall be in you" is not the Body's indwelling doctrine).
- Luke 21 and Matthew 24 are read with the standing rulings ("one taken, one left" is Israel's Tribulation separation; Luke 21 states the AD 70 and future sections).
- Mode 2 permits argument and identification; every identification follows the Identity Synthesis Protocol.
- No internal working term appears in anything the reader sees (Rule 14).
