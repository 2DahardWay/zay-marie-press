- **Scope:** five volumes, each walking one section of the book of Acts in order, so the series reads as a commentary on Acts through the two-program lens. Where a volume enters ground already argued in another series, it cites that volume as a neighbour and does not repeat the argument.
- **Length:** 50–70 pages including the cover. **Imprint:** Zay-Marie Press. **Translation:** KJV only. **Typeface:** Roboto.
- **Price:** $14.99 per volume unless the publisher says otherwise.
- **Footer:** "ZAY-MARIE PRESS ACTS COMMENTARY SERIES, VOLUME V • N" (page number restarts at 1 on the Table of Contents page).
- **Cover:** "VOLUME V" upper right, the title, and "ACTS COMMENTARY SERIES" at the bottom; 1600 × 2400 px artwork (2:3), 1024 × 1536 px website covers (.jpg and .webp), full-bleed interior cover page. Artwork supplied by the publisher; Claude removes any badge and sets any missing lettering.

## The volumes (titles and order ruled 2026-10-08)
1. **Acts 1–8: What the Twelve Were Told, and Did** — the question of Acts 1:6 and its answer, the choosing of Matthias, Pentecost, the Temple preaching of chapter 3, the council's rejection, Stephen, the scattering, Samaria. Narrower question: what does the book report Israel being offered, refusing and being given further time, scene by scene?
2. **The Spirit in Acts: Pentecost to Ephesus** — Acts 2, 8, 10 and 19 set side by side with the order of each coming. Narrower question: what pattern does the Spirit's coming follow in each setting, and what does the text say about the audience of each? (Heaviest overlap: Studies 20, 34, 38, so this volume reads the four scenes in order rather than arguing the general rule.)
3. **Paul Called and Sent: Acts 9, 13, 22 and 26** — the three accounts of the road to Damascus compared word for word, the sending from Antioch, and the commission as Paul reports it. Narrower question: what does each account add or omit, and what does that show about the stewardship committed to him?
4. **Cornelius and the Council: Acts 10–15** — Peter's vision, Cornelius, the report at Jerusalem, Antioch, the first journey's close, the Council's question and answer. Narrower question: what exactly was asked at Jerusalem, and who answered which part?
5. **Acts 28: The Close of the Book** — Rome, the elders of the Jews, Isaiah 6, "the hope of Israel," the two years, and what the book leaves unsaid. Narrower question: what does the text of Acts 28 report, and what does it not report?

Volumes are numbered 1–5 within the series. Each volume's thesis, outline and rulings are approved before drafting.

## Drafting cautions
- Acts 9–28 is the overlap: report the text; fillings, signs and events are not assigned to a single program (Rule 11); Paul's synagogue preaching is audience-specific, not commission-specific (Rule 18); the remnant and the Body stay distinct (Rule 17).
- Acts 28 is the national suspension of Israel's program, not a transfer (Rules 13 and 17).
- Mode 2 permits argument and identification; every identification follows the Identity Synthesis Protocol.
- No internal working term appears in anything the reader sees (Rule 14).
