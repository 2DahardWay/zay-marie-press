# Prophets Series Standard

Version 1.0 — DRAFT, 2026-10-15, recorded in the repository with the publisher's "i approve" (2026-10-15). Approval is for keeping the draft on record only: every item marked proposed or pending below remains unruled, and no volume work, website page or pricing follows until the publisher rules on them. The publisher chose the Prophets Series as the ninth series, five volumes, from the gap analysis (handoff, 2026-10-15). Everything below marked **proposed** is Claude's drafting from the sibling Standards and is not ruled. Volume titles, narrower questions and passage lists are provisional; the publisher approves title, subtitle, thesis, outline and rulings one volume at a time. Nothing is on the website, in CLAUDE.md, in the Master Standard or in the Framework yet. On approval the series is recorded in Master Standard §20 Rule 29 (a new DOCX version, PDF and Version History entry) and in CLAUDE.md in the same pass, and the Framework §XX wording is supplied to the publisher for his copy.

Governs the Prophets Series (each title a "Volume"; the shared working term "Commentary Volume" is used for build tooling only), written in Mode 2 (canonical identity synthesis) under Framework §XX and Master Standard §20 Rule 29. Modelled on the Pauline Doctrine Series Standard (v1.15), the Gospel Commentary Series Standard (v1.19) and the Acts Commentary Series Standard (v1.10), and sharing their layout, quotation and citation rules. The Digital Studies stay under Mode 1 and are never changed by this series.

- **Pricing (open, the publisher's decision):** the existing pattern would put the Complete Commentary Library at $409.99 for 45 volumes. Not ruled until he confirms it.

## Proposed (not yet ruled)
- **Why a topic series, and not a walk through the prophetic books:** every prophetic book already has a Bible Book Companion (all 66 are built; the pages still carry the former Doctrinal Packet file names), and the largest single passages are already argued elsewhere: Daniel 9 (Prophetic Commentary Volume 4, Study 10), Isaiah 7:13–16 (Study 63), the Servant in Isaiah 41–53 (Study 86), the day of the LORD and Gog and Magog (Study 7, Prophetic Commentary Volume 5), the remnant (Study 9, Prophetic Identity Volume 2), the New Covenant (Study 12, Prophetic Identity Volume 3), Ezekiel 40–48 (Typology Volume 1), the second exodus (Typology Volume 4) and the Davidic throne (Covenantal Volume 5, Study 73). A series organised by book would be thin and would collide with all of these, for the same reason the Pauline Doctrine Series was organised by topic and not by letter. The series is therefore five volumes, each built around one narrower question the prophets themselves raise.
- **Name:** Prophets Series (a topic series, not "commentary"). Near-name caution: the Commentary Collection already carries the Prophetic Commentary Series (Revelation and Daniel) and the Prophetic Identity Commentary Series. The Prophets Series differs in subject (the Old Testament prophets as prophets, not the final ruler or Israel's identity), but the three names sit close in the series strip and jump bar. Recommendation: keep "Prophets Series" and set the anchor `#prophets`; if the publisher prefers a name that cannot be confused, the alternative is "The Prophets Speak".
- **Placement:** the Commentary Collection page (`commentary.html#prophets`) as the **ninth** series, outside the Digital Studies catalog, bundles and Library Edition. No separate series page. Volume slugs: `commentary-prophets-0N-<slug>.html`. Series strip, jump bar, which-series row, index and bundle row follow the Pauline block. The $54.99 bundle and the 45-volume Library are held until the series is complete, so the Library box keeps showing the current count and price until then.
- **Order:** the five volumes in the order listed below; no individual Digital Studies are planned with this series.

## Proposed wording (built from the sibling Standards)
- **Scope:** five volumes on the Old Testament prophets, each built around one narrower question, not a walk through a book. Where a volume enters ground already argued in another series or in a Digital Study, it cites that work as a neighbour and does not repeat the argument.
- **Length:** 50–70 pages including the cover. **Imprint:** Zay-Marie Press. **Translation:** KJV only. **Typeface:** Roboto.
- **Price:** $14.99 per volume unless the publisher says otherwise. Series bundle $54.99; Complete Commentary Library for 45 volumes $409.99 (his decision; see above).
- **Footer:** "ZAY-MARIE PRESS PROPHETS SERIES, VOLUME V • N" (V the volume number within the series, N the page number, restarting at 1 on the Table of Contents page).
- **Cover:** "VOLUME V" upper right, the title, and "PROPHETS SERIES" at the bottom; 1600 × 2400 px artwork (2:3), 1024 × 1536 px website covers (.jpg and .webp), full-bleed interior cover page. Artwork supplied by the publisher; Claude removes any badge, sets any missing lettering and shows proofs before the cover is approved.
- **Citation and sources:** passage citation (book, chapter, verse or section); modern sources only (twentieth century and later; no author from before 1900 is cited, quoted or listed in a bibliography); a page number is printed only where the text was read in full; a writer is quoted only from a free full text read, otherwise described in Claude's own words without quotation marks; verse-level index "Where the Passages Are Read" as the last appendix before the Bibliography (Master Standard §20 Rule 29).
- **Preview pages:** four, as Master Standard §20 Rule 29 fixes: Table of Contents, Preface, one page of the argument at work and one page of objections and replies, rendered from the final PDF at 1041 × 1560 px and labelled by interior page number; shown to the publisher and approved before any push.
- **Binding rules:** Rules 7, 11 and 13–19 bind; Rules 8, 12, 20–28 and 30 do not. **Rule 32** (Romans 9–11 and the Romans 10:9–10 formula) binds every volume, so it applies wherever a prophetic text is quoted in Romans 9–11 (Hosea 1–2, Isaiah 10, 28, 29, 59 and Joel 2:32 among them). Standing sentences are carried wherever the passage is touched: Galatians 3 ("The seed is prophetic fulfillment in Christ; the Body receives blessing through Christ alone, not through participation in the Abrahamic covenant."); Rule 17 (the remnant and the Body); Rule 18 (Paul's synagogue ministry); Rule 19 (ground of salvation and content of faith); the Romans 11 triad ("Participation is not identity. Blessing is not covenant. Standing is not program membership."). **Rule 33** (Signs after Acts 28) is not in the Master Standard's Mode 2 binding list; because Volume 4 treats Elijah's signs and the signs of the two witnesses, the standing sentence is carried there as the Framework's own ruling, and the publisher is asked to confirm that reading.
- **Reader-facing text:** a volume opens with a plain statement that it argues a position. The mode label, the Framework, rule numbers and every other working term never appear in anything the reader sees (Rule 14), including text inside diagrams, covers and preview images (OCR pass).
- **Objections chapter:** the Covenantal pattern (two to four modern writers who fortify the thesis beside named modern writers who hold the opposite view, each objection in the opponent's own words, short extracts checked against a free full text, answered from Scripture, closing table).
- **Build toolkit:** `build-tools/commentary/` (build.py, content.py, kjvlib.py, kjvcheck.py, tocmap.py, mkcover.py, mk.sh). KJV text is verified from a source and checked by hash with `kjvcheck.py` before publication.

## The volumes (provisional titles; the publisher approves title, subtitle, thesis, outline and rulings one volume at a time)
1. **How the Prophets Spoke** — the office, the test and the conditional word. Numbers 12:6–8; Deuteronomy 13:1–5; 18:15–22; 1 Samuel 3:19–21; 1 Kings 22; Jeremiah 18:7–10; 23:9–40; 26:12–19; 28; Ezekiel 13; 33:13–16; Jonah 3–4; Amos 3:7; Hebrews 1:1; 1 Peter 1:10–12; 2 Peter 1:19–21. Narrower question: by the prophets' own statements, how is a word of the LORD known, what can turn an oracle aside, and what does the LORD swear that cannot be turned? Foundation for the series.
2. **What the Prophets Condemned** — sacrifice, the Sabbath and the heart. 1 Samuel 15:22; Psalm 40:6; 50:7–15; 51:16–17; Isaiah 1:10–20; 58; 66:1–4; Jeremiah 6:20; 7:1–15, 21–23; Hosea 6:6; Amos 4:4–5; 5:21–27; Micah 6:6–8; Zechariah 7; Malachi 1:6–14; 3:1–4; Matthew 9:13; 12:7; Hebrews 10:5–10. Narrower question: what does each prophet condemn, in whose worship, and what does he leave standing?
3. **The Nations Judged** — Babylon, Tyre, Edom, Egypt and Nineveh. Isaiah 13–23; Jeremiah 25:15–38; 46–51; Ezekiel 25–32; Amos 1:3–2:3; Obadiah; Nahum; Zephaniah 2; Habakkuk 2:6–20; Zechariah 9:1–8; Genesis 12:3. Narrower question: which oracles does Scripture itself report fulfilled, which does it hold open, and what does each say it rests on?
4. **Elijah Must First Come** — Malachi, John the Baptist and the two witnesses. 1 Kings 17–19; 2 Kings 1–2; Isaiah 40:3; Malachi 3:1–4:6; Luke 1:13–17; 7:24–28; Matthew 3:1–3; 11:7–15; 17:9–13; Mark 1:2–8; 9:11–13; John 1:19–28; 3:28–30; Revelation 11:3–12. Narrower question: what do Malachi 3:1 and 4:5–6 promise, how do the Gospels answer, and what does the text say of a further coming?
5. **Daniel's Kingdoms** — the statue, the ram and the goat, and the king of the north. Daniel 2; 8; 10:1–12:13. Narrower question: where does each of these visions place its own break between what is past and what is awaited, and what does the text say that break rests on? Daniel 7 and Daniel 9 stay with Prophetic Commentary Volumes 2 and 4 and are cited, not re-argued.

Volumes are numbered 1–5 within the series. Each volume's thesis, outline and rulings are approved before drafting.

## Held back (ranked ideas not in the five)
Hebrews and the General Letters; Revelation beyond the Prophetic series; The Unseen World (Isaiah 14:12–15 and Ezekiel 28:11–19 touch it and are left out of Volume 3's identifications until that series is decided); Beginnings (Genesis 1–11); Positions Compared. Alternates for Volume 5 if the closer check shows too much overlap: The Twelve as One Book (the order and movement of Hosea to Malachi), or The Return the Prophets Promised, narrowed to what was met under Cyrus and what is held for "the second time" (Isaiah 11:11). The Relationship Map shows this is the most crowded of the three: Study 7 traces the prophetic Day's judgment and Israel's restoration, Study 12 Israel's restoration under Messiah, Study 53 Israel's national redemption (Isaiah 59:20), Study 49 Ezekiel 37's national restoration, Studies 21 and 56 touch it, and Typology Volume 4 and Prophetic Identity Volumes 2 and 3 carry the second-exodus, remnant and covenant passages. The return under Cyrus is treated only in passing (Prophetic Commentary Volume 4), which is the one opening. The order between Daniel's Kingdoms and the Return is undecided until the neighbouring PDFs are read.

## Cautions for drafting (proposed)
- **Volume 1:** Acts 3:19–21 is read as the Acts Commentary Standard rules it (Israel's call to repent for the times of refreshing, not set beside the Mystery); the volume builds no doctrine of a kingdom offered and withdrawn beyond that ruling. Covenantal Volume 1 argues the Abrahamic covenant is unconditional, so the conditional word of Jeremiah 18:7–10 is read as the prophets' rule for announced judgment and blessing and is never set against that covenant. Rule 19 governs wherever the content of faith is named (Rule 23 is Mode 1 and does not bind).
- **Volume 2:** Hebrews 10:5–10 is read within Israel's Prophecy line on its own audience markers and the covenant it quotes, with the Body benefiting from Christ's blood without being the covenant party; Ezekiel 40–48 stays with Typology Volume 1; Malachi 3:8–12 stays with Study 47.
- **Volume 3:** Isaiah 14:12–15 and Ezekiel 28:11–19 are not identified with any unseen person here (the Identity Synthesis Protocol would require a ruling, and that ground belongs to the held-back series); Revelation 17–18 stay with Prophetic Commentary Volume 3.
- **Volume 4:** the identification of the two witnesses is a Mode 2 identification and goes through the Identity Synthesis Protocol, with the publisher's ruling before drafting; John 1:21 and Matthew 11:14 are each read in their own setting; Rule 33's sentence is carried wherever signs are treated.
- **Volume 5:** the Rule 29 required conclusion that the Revelation 13 beast is Daniel's little horn stands and is cited, not re-argued; Study 74's report-only treatment of the final-ruler passages is not changed; Antiochus and the end-time ruler are read from the text of Daniel 8 and 11, with the preterist and critical readings answered in the objections chapter.
