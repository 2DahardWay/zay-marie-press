### Study 49 — The Resurrections of Scripture — CONFLICT (website wording)

- **Where:** `study-49-the-resurrections-of-scripture.html`, "About This Study", third paragraph.
- **Study says:** "nor of the rapture passages in Matthew 24 and Luke 17, which Study 29 traces in their own setting."

The Study 49 related-studies entry for Study 29 already uses the correct framing ("the prophetic separation traced there").
- **Scope on the website:** the phrase appears in this one place only. It is not in `study-connections.html` or `digital-studies.html`.
- **Not yet checked:** whether the interior PDF of Study 49 carries the same sentence.
- **Fix (approved by publisher to make; applied locally, NOT pushed):** replace "the rapture passages in Matthew 24 and Luke 17" with "the 'one taken, one left' passages in Matthew 24 and Luke 17".
