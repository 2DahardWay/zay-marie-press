# Typology Commentary Series Standard

Version 1.0 — publisher directive, 2026-10-08. Governs the Typology Commentary Series (each title a "Commentary Volume"), written in Mode 2 (canonical identity synthesis) under Framework §XX and Master Standard §20 Rule 29. It is modelled on the Prophecy & Mystery Commentary Series Standard (v1.8) and shares its layout, quotation and citation rules. The Digital Studies stay under Mode 1 and the Master Standard; nothing here changes them. This Standard records the series and introduces no new rule, so the Master Standard stays at v1.76.

## Ruled by the publisher (2026-10-08)
- **Name:** Typology Commentary Series. Each title is a Commentary Volume.
- **Scope:** five volumes, one typology per volume, each read in its own canonical setting.
- **Length:** 50–70 pages including the cover.
- **Imprint:** Zay-Marie Press.
- **Translation:** KJV only.
- **Typeface:** Roboto.
- **Price:** $14.99 per volume unless the publisher says otherwise.
- **Footer:** "ZAY-MARIE PRESS TYPOLOGY COMMENTARY SERIES, VOLUME V • N" (V = volume number, N = page number; the page number restarts at 1 on the Table of Contents page).
- **Cover:** "VOLUME V" at the upper right, the title, and "TYPOLOGY COMMENTARY SERIES" at the bottom. 1600 × 2400 px artwork (2:3), 1024 × 1536 px website covers (.jpg and .webp), full-bleed interior cover page. Artwork is supplied by the publisher; Claude removes any "Made with AI" badge and sets any missing lettering.

- **Catalog placement:** shown on the Commentary Collection page (`commentary.html#typology`) outside the Digital Studies catalog, bundles and library count. No separate series page.
- **Binding rules:** Rules 7, 11 and 13–19 bind (including the standing Galatians 3 and Romans 11 sentences and Rule 18); Rules 8, 12 and 20–28 do not.

## Volume 1 — Tabernacle & Temple Typology (thesis, outline and rulings approved 2026-10-08)
**Title and subtitle.** Tabernacle & Temple Typology — The Pattern Shown in the Mount, Its Fulfilment in Christ, and Where It Does Not Pass to the Body.

**Thesis.** God gave Moses a pattern (Exodus 25:9, 40). Scripture calls it a shadow, copy and pattern of heavenly things (Hebrews 8:5; 9:23–24; 10:1; Colossians 2:17), and its parts point to Christ. The tabernacle and temples belong to Israel's program, fulfilled in Christ and in the Kingdom temple. The Body's temple language (1 Corinthians 3:16; 6:19; Ephesians 2:21–22) is Mystery content, neither a fulfilment nor a transfer of Israel's types.

**Outline.** Preface; Thesis; ch. 1 what a type is; ch. 2 the pattern at Sinai; ch. 3 court, altar, laver; ch. 4 the Holy Place; ch. 5 the Holy of Holies, veil and Day of Atonement; ch. 6 the temple, from Solomon to the temple of His body; ch. 7 Hebrews 8–10 in order; ch. 8 Paul's temple language for the Body; ch. 9 the temples to come; ch. 10 objections and replies; Conclusion; Appendices A–F (A The Tabernacle at a Glance, B The Words, C Questions for Study, D Terms, E The Key Passages in Full, F Where the Passages Are Read); Bibliography.

**Rulings (publisher, 2026-10-08):**
1. Type method: firm where Scripture names it (Hebrews, Colossians, John); echoes marked as argued.
2. The Body's temple language is separate from Israel's types; no transfer.
3. Ezekiel 40–48 is a literal Kingdom temple with memorial and ritual sacrifices; Christ's sacrifice alone removes sin.
4. The seventieth-week temple is a rebuilt temple in Jerusalem (Daniel 9:27; Matthew 24:15; 2 Thessalonians 2:4; Revelation 11:1–2); the new Jerusalem has no temple (Revelation 21:22).
5. The rent veil (Matthew 27:51) is read with Hebrews 10:20; the earthly sanctuary is the copy and the heavenly one "the true"; priesthood is left to Volume 3.
6. Hebrews takes the reader-facing line on its audience markers and the Jeremiah 31 covenant.

**Voices.** Quoted from free full texts: Thomas Ice (two Pre-Trib Research Center articles), Steve Gregg (Christian Research Journal 35:3, 2012), Vern S. Poythress (*The Shadow of Christ in the Law of Moses*, ch. 2). G. K. Beale (*The Temple and the Church's Mission*; with Mitchell Kim, *God Dwells Among Us*) described in the volume's own words, no quotation.

**Status.** Interior approved 2026-10-08 (50 pages including the cover; Appendix E 37 passages; Appendix F 212 rows). Supplied cover approved 2026-10-08 (badge removed, "VOLUME 1" lettered). Page `commentary-typology-01-tabernacle-and-temple-typology.html`.
